## FAQ

### Is the NAPLEX adaptive like the NCLEX?

No. Per NABP, the NAPLEX is a **computerized fixed-form** exam (225 questions, 6 hours of exam time — confirm current Candidate Application Bulletin). You generally cannot skip and return after submitting an item.

### What’s the biggest content domain?

**Person-Centered Assessment and Treatment Planning (~40%)** on the Content Outline effective May 1, 2025+. Foundational Knowledge and Medication Use Process are ~25% each.

### Do I need a separate law exam?

Usually **yes** — many jurisdictions require the **MPJE** (or state equivalent) in addition to NAPLEX. This book does **not** replace MPJE study.

### How long should I study?

It depends on how fresh your therapeutics and calculations are. Many candidates use **4–8 weeks** of structured review after APPE-heavy seasons; shorter intensives work if content is already strong. Consistency + daily questions + calc practice beat cramming.

### Is this book enough without a question bank?

No — and that’s intentional. The book builds frameworks and high-yield judgment; **practice builds exam performance**. Pair with a strong Qbank and the official NABP outline/bulletin.
