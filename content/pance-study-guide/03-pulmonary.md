# Pulmonary (9%)

**Top 5 high-yield**

1. Asthma: no "rescue inhaler only" treatment
2. COPD: diagnosis and first inhalers
3. Outpatient pneumonia antibiotics
4. PE workup
5. Lung cancer screening

**Abbreviations:** SABA = short-acting rescue inhaler (e.g., albuterol). ICS = inhaled steroid. LABA / LAMA = long-acting bronchodilators. FEV₁/FVC = breathing-test ratio. CAP = community-acquired pneumonia. PE = pulmonary embolism. CTPA = CT pulmonary angiogram. LDCT = low-dose CT. PERC = PE rule-out criteria. V/Q = ventilation-perfusion scan.

**Checklist**

- [ ] **Asthma (teens and adults): never treat with a SABA alone.** [C13]
- [ ] **Preferred asthma reliever:** low-dose ICS-formoterol, as needed (GINA "Track 1"). [C13]
- [ ] **COPD diagnosis:** post-bronchodilator **FEV₁/FVC <0.70**. [C10]
- [ ] **COPD, Groups B and E (first treatment):** LABA + LAMA. [C11]
- [ ] **Group E with eosinophils ≥300:** consider LABA + LAMA + ICS. [C11]
- [ ] **LABA + ICS alone is not encouraged in COPD.** [C11]
- [ ] **COPD flare:** prednisone 40 mg a day for 5 days. [C12]
- [ ] **Outpatient CAP, healthy adult:** amoxicillin, *or* doxycycline, *or* a macrolide (only if local resistance is <25%). [C14]
- [ ] **Lung cancer screening:** yearly LDCT, ages 50–80, ≥20 pack-years, smoking now or quit within 15 years. [C15]
- [ ] **PE, low pretest probability:** D-dimer first. Negative = PE ruled out. Positive → CTPA or V/Q scan. [C16]
- [ ] **ED patient with low probability:** PERC rule can skip the D-dimer. [C16]
- [ ] **Over age 50:** age-adjusted D-dimer cut-off (age × 10 µg/L) is safe. [C16]
- [ ] **PE/DVT treatment (first 3 months):** DOAC over warfarin. *Exception:* antiphospholipid syndrome → warfarin. [C17]
- [ ] **Bronchiolitis (infant):** do **not** give albuterol. [C18]
- [ ] **Latent TB:** short rifamycin regimens (3HP or 4R) are preferred. [C19]

**Look-alike: asthma vs COPD**

| Feature | Asthma | COPD |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typical patient | Younger, allergies | Older, smoking history |
| Breathing test | Reversible | FEV₁/FVC <0.70 after bronchodilator [C10] |
| Starting treatment | ICS-formoterol as needed [C13] | LABA + LAMA (Groups B/E) [C11] |

> **Important to remember**
> - **Trap:** "albuterol only" for asthma is wrong under GINA 2026.
> - **Trap:** D-dimer when probability is high. Go to imaging.
