# Exam day + final sprint

### 7-day sprint

| Day | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| −7 | Full 5-block mock + error log |
| −6 | Cardio + Pulm weak spots |
| −5 | GI + MSK + ID |
| −4 | Neuro + Psych + Reproductive |
| −3 | Peds and surgery items + Professional Practice |
| −2 | Quick refs only |
| −1 | Light review, logistics, sleep |

### Exam-day checklist

- [ ] **Valid ID** that meets current NCCPA/Pearson VUE rules (check before test day)
- [ ] Arrive early
- [ ] **Plan your 45 min of breaks** (e.g., a short one after each block)
- [ ] About **1 minute per question**. Mark and move.
- [ ] Each item: **diagnosis, next step, or treatment?**
- [ ] Eat and breathe between blocks
- [ ] After: check your NCCPA account for results

## The day itself

**The night before.** Stop opening new topics. A light pass over the quick-reference tables is enough. Lay out the identification that matches the current NCCPA and Pearson VUE rules. Confirm the test center address and the time you need to leave. Eat a normal meal. The score is not improved by an all-night review.

**At the center.** You will be checked in. Personal items stay outside the testing room. The exam is five blocks. You have 45 minutes of break time to spend as you choose, and the clock for a block does not pause because you are thinking. Plan a short break after each block rather than saving the entire 45 minutes for the end, when you are too tired to use it. About one minute per question is the pace that finishes a block. If a stem is not yielding, mark it and move. A blank item is a miss. A guessed item after a stuck re-read is often a miss too.

**How to read the item.** Name the task before the diagnosis: is this history, the most likely diagnosis, the next test, the treatment, or a professional-practice rule? The NCCPA blueprint effective January 2025 scores every medical item on both an organ system and a task. Unstable patients are stabilized first. Pregnancy, kidney function, and allergy are checked before a drug is chosen.

**After you leave.** Do not reconstruct the whole exam in the parking lot. Results are released on the schedule NCCPA publishes. A practice-test percentage is not a PANCE score. Use the score report, when you have one, to see which systems and tasks were thin.

**How to spend the 45 minutes.** Five blocks of 60 questions, 60 minutes each, is the NCCPA structure (see the facts table in the back of this book). A short break after block 1 and block 3 leaves enough time for a longer break after block 4, when fatigue changes answers. Do not skip the tutorial clock and then discover you have less break than you planned. Eat something you have eaten before. A new snack is an unnecessary variable.

**If a block starts badly.** The first five items do not predict the block. Mark a stem you cannot parse in under a minute and return if time remains. Changing a completed answer in the last two minutes, without a new fact from the stem, is the late-block trap in the previous chapter. The task weights on the January 2025 blueprint (diagnosis 18%, history 16%, clinical intervention 16%, pharmacotherapy 15%) mean a bad run of drug items is not a bad run of the whole exam.
