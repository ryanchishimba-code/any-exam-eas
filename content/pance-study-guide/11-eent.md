# EENT (6%)

**Top 5 high-yield**

1. Strep throat: test, then penicillin/amoxicillin
2. Ear infection: high-dose amoxicillin
3. Red-eye emergencies
4. Vision loss emergencies
5. Nosebleeds

**Abbreviations:** GAS = group A strep. RADT = rapid strep test. AOM = acute otitis media.

**Checklist**

- [ ] **Sore throat:** use a clinical score to decide who to test. [C51]
- [ ] **Confirmed strep:** penicillin or amoxicillin × **10 days**. [C51]
- [ ] **AOM needing antibiotics:** **high-dose amoxicillin** (80–90 mg/kg/day). [C52]

**Look-alike: red eye (classic teaching points)**

| Disease | Classic clue | Urgent? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conjunctivitis | Discharge, normal vision | No |
| Acute angle-closure glaucoma | Pain, halos, mid-dilated pupil | **Yes** |
| Corneal abrasion | Foreign-body feeling, stain uptake | Usually no |

> **Important to remember:** **Painful red eye + vision change = emergency** until proven otherwise.
