# Professional Practice (6%)

**Top 5 high-yield**

1. Decision-making capacity
2. Informed consent
3. Confidentiality (HIPAA)
4. Telling patients about errors
5. Minors and consent

**Checklist (core concepts; follow your state law and employer policy)**

- [ ] **Capacity** = the patient can understand, appreciate, reason, and state a choice.
- [ ] **Informed consent** covers risks, benefits, and alternatives (including no treatment).
- [ ] **Share health information** only as HIPAA allows.
- [ ] **Medical errors:** be honest with the patient; report through your system.

> **Important to remember:** **Capacity is clinical and decision-specific.** Competence is a legal ruling.
