# Musculoskeletal (8%)

**Top 5 high-yield**

1. Ottawa ankle rules
2. Gout: flare vs long-term treatment
3. Rheumatoid arthritis first drug
4. Osteoporosis screening
5. Red flags in back pain

**Abbreviations:** ULT = urate-lowering therapy. RA = rheumatoid arthritis. OA = osteoarthritis. DMARD = disease-modifying drug. DXA = bone density scan.

**Look-alike: joint disease (classic teaching points)**

| Feature | OA | RA | Gout |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Morning stiffness | <30 min | >1 hour | Varies |
| Typical joints | Knees, hips, DIP | MCP, PIP, wrists | Big toe |
| Pattern | Wear and tear | Symmetric, autoimmune | Sudden, red, hot |

> **Important to remember**
> - **Ottawa rules decide who needs an X-ray.**
