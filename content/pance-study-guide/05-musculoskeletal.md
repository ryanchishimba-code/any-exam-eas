# Musculoskeletal (8%)

**Top 5 high-yield**

1. Ottawa ankle rules
2. Gout: flare vs long-term treatment
3. Rheumatoid arthritis first drug
4. Osteoporosis screening
5. Red flags in back pain

**Abbreviations:** ULT = urate-lowering therapy. RA = rheumatoid arthritis. OA = osteoarthritis. DMARD = disease-modifying drug. DXA = bone density scan.

**Checklist**

- [ ] **Ottawa ankle X-ray** if pain near the ankle bones *plus* any: tenderness at the back edge/tip of either malleolus, or can't take 4 steps (right after injury *and* in the ED). [C24]
- [ ] **Ottawa foot X-ray:** tenderness at the base of the 5th metatarsal or the navicular, or can't take 4 steps. [C24]
- [ ] **Gout flare:** colchicine (low dose), NSAIDs, or steroids. [C25]
- [ ] **Gout long-term:** allopurinol first-line; target uric acid **<6 mg/dL**. [C25]
- [ ] **RA, moderate–high activity:** methotrexate alone is the first DMARD. [C27]
- [ ] **Osteoporosis screening (DXA):** all women **≥65**. [C26]
- [ ] **Postmenopausal women <65:** screen if ≥1 risk factor *and* a risk tool shows higher risk. [C26]
- [ ] **Men:** evidence is insufficient (USPSTF "I" statement). [C26]

**Look-alike: joint disease (classic teaching points)**

| Feature | OA | RA | Gout |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Morning stiffness | <30 min | >1 hour | Varies |
| Typical joints | Knees, hips, DIP | MCP, PIP, wrists | Big toe |
| Pattern | Wear and tear | Symmetric, autoimmune | Sudden, red, hot |

> **Important to remember**
> - **Don't start allopurinol as "flare treatment."** It's long-term therapy. [C25]
> - **Ottawa rules decide who needs an X-ray.**
