# FAQ

### How many questions are on PANCE, and how long is it?
300 multiple-choice questions in 5 blocks of 60. You get 60 minutes per block, 45 minutes of total break time, and a 15-minute tutorial. Plan for about six hours.

### What score do I need to pass PANCE?
PANCE is scored from 200 to 800. The passing score is 350.

### Which PANCE topics are weighted most?
Cardiovascular (11%), Pulmonary (9%), GI/Nutrition (8%), and Musculoskeletal (8%). By task, "most likely diagnosis" is the biggest at 18%.

### How long should I study for PANCE?
Most people plan 2 to 12 weeks. Choose based on how fresh your content is. Daily timed practice matters most.

### How many times can I take PANCE?
Up to six times within six years. You can test once every 90 days, and no more than three times a year.

### What's the difference between PANCE, PANRE, and PANRE-LA?
PANCE is your first certification after PA school. PANRE and PANRE-LA are recertification options for certified PAs. PANRE is a single test-center exam. PANRE-LA is answered online over several quarters.

### Can AnyExamEasy help me prepare for PANCE?
This book is a study aid. It is independent and not affiliated with NCCPA. Practice items are a separate part of preparation. The book does not promise a score.

## Answers that belong with the blueprint

### Do task weights change how I study a disease?
Yes. A cardiology item can be a history question, a drug question, or a basic-science question. The NCCPA blueprint effective January 2025 gives formulating the most likely diagnosis 18%, history and physical examination 16%, clinical intervention 16%, and pharmaceutical therapeutics 15%. Studying only the drug list misses the larger share.

### Where do the numbers in this book come from?
Blood pressure statements follow the 2025 AHA/ACC guideline. Lipid statements follow the 2026 ACC/AHA dyslipidemia guideline. Acute kidney injury statements follow KDIGO 2024. Streptococcal pharyngitis follows IDSA 2012. Acute otitis media follows the AAP 2013 guideline. If a line does not name a source, treat it as a study prompt and open the guideline before you memorize a dose.

### What if two answers both seem right?
Read the verb again. Initial test, best test, and first-line treatment are different. If the patient is unstable, stabilize first. Then check pregnancy, kidney function, and allergy. The choice that matches the verb, and does not violate one of those three constraints, is the one the item is scoring.
