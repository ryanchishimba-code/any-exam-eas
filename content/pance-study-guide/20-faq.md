# FAQ

### How many questions are on PANCE, and how long is it?
300 multiple-choice questions in 5 blocks of 60. You get 60 minutes per block, 45 minutes of total break time, and a 15-minute tutorial. Plan for about six hours.

### What score do I need to pass PANCE?
PANCE is scored from 200 to 800. The passing score is 350.

### Which PANCE topics are weighted most?
Cardiovascular (11%), Pulmonary (9%), GI/Nutrition (8%), and Musculoskeletal (8%). By task, "most likely diagnosis" is the biggest at 18%.

### How long should I study for PANCE?
Most people plan 2 to 12 weeks. Choose based on how fresh your content is. Daily timed practice matters most.

### How many times can I take PANCE?
Up to six times within six years. You can test once every 90 days, and no more than three times a year.

### What's the difference between PANCE, PANRE, and PANRE-LA?
PANCE is your first certification after PA school. PANRE and PANRE-LA are recertification options for certified PAs. PANRE is a single test-center exam. PANRE-LA is answered online over several quarters.
