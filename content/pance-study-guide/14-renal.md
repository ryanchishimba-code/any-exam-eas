# Renal (5%)

**Top 5 high-yield**

1. AKI definition
2. CKD definition and stages
3. Potassium emergencies
4. Sodium problems
5. Kidney stones

**Abbreviations:** AKI = acute kidney injury. CKD = chronic kidney disease. GFR = kidney filtering rate. ACR = urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio.

**Checklist**

- [ ] **AKI (KDIGO):** creatinine up **≥0.3 mg/dL in 48 h**, *or* **≥1.5× baseline in 7 days**, *or* urine **<0.5 mL/kg/h for 6 h**. [C54]
- [ ] **CKD (KDIGO):** kidney damage or low function lasting **>3 months**. [C55]
- [ ] **GFR <60** = stage G3a or worse. Albuminuria starts at **ACR ≥30 mg/g**. [C55]

> **Important to remember:** **CKD needs 3 months.** One bad creatinine is not CKD.
