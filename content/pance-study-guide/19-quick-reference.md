# Quick refs

**Screening (USPSTF)**

![Age bars for USPSTF screening: depression 12 and up, anxiety 8 to 64, HIV 15 to 65, cervical 21 to 65, breast 40 to 74, colorectal 45 to 75, diabetes 35 to 70, lung 50 to 80, PSA 55 to 69, AAA 65 to 75, osteoporosis 65 and up.](visuals/14-uspstf-screening-timeline.svg)

*Figure: USPSTF screening by age.*


| Test | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Colorectal | 45–75 |
| Breast | Every 2 years, 40–74 |
| Cervical | 21–65 (see table above) |
| Lung (LDCT) | 50–80, ≥20 pack-years |
| AAA | Men 65–75 who ever smoked |
| HIV | 15–65 |
| Depression | Adults + teens 12–18 |
| Diabetes | 35–70 with overweight/obesity |
| Osteoporosis | Women ≥65 (+ some younger) |
| PSA | Men 55–69: shared decision |

**"First test" pairs**

| Situation | First step |
| --- | --- |
| Chest pain | ECG |
| Stroke symptoms | Non-contrast head CT |
| Thunderclap headache | Non-contrast head CT |
| Ankle injury | Apply Ottawa rules |
| Low-probability PE | D-dimer |
| Possible ectopic | Pregnancy test + TVUS |
