# Hematologic (5%)

**Top 5 high-yield**

1. Iron deficiency diagnosis
2. Microcytic anemia look-alikes
3. Platelet disorders
4. Clotting tests
5. Leukemia/lymphoma clues

**Abbreviations:** MCV = mean corpuscular volume (average red-cell size).

**Checklist**

- [ ] **Iron deficiency:** ferritin **<45 ng/mL** in an anemic patient (AGA 2020). [C53]
- [ ] **Ferritin can be falsely normal** with inflammation or CKD. [C53]


![Anemia by MCV: microcytic under 80 (iron deficiency, thalassemia trait, chronic disease); normocytic 80 to 100 (blood loss, chronic disease, CKD, hemolysis); macrocytic over 100 (B12 or folate deficiency, alcohol or liver disease, drugs).](visuals/13-anemia-mcv.svg)

*Figure: Anemia by MCV (teaching aid; iron cut-off AGA 2020).*

**Look-alike: small red cells (microcytic) (classic teaching points)**

| Disease | Ferritin | Key clue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Iron deficiency | Low | Blood loss, heavy periods |
| Thalassemia trait | Normal | Family history, mild anemia |
| Anemia of chronic disease | Normal/high | Chronic illness |

> **Important to remember:** **Iron deficiency in an older adult → look for GI blood loss.**
