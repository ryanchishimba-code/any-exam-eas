# Endocrine (6%)

**Top 5 high-yield**

1. Diabetes diagnosis numbers
2. Type 2 diabetes drug choice by comorbidity
3. DKA criteria and potassium rule
4. Diabetes screening
5. Hypothyroidism treatment

**Abbreviations:** A1C = 3-month sugar average. FPG = fasting glucose. OGTT = glucose tolerance test. GLP-1 RA = GLP-1 receptor agonist. SGLT2i = SGLT2 inhibitor. DKA = diabetic ketoacidosis. TSH = thyroid-stimulating hormone.

**Diabetes diagnosis (ADA 2026) [C46]**

| Test | Diabetes |
| --- | --- |
| A1C | ≥6.5% |
| Fasting glucose | ≥126 mg/dL |
| 2-hour OGTT | ≥200 mg/dL |
| Random glucose + symptoms | ≥200 mg/dL |

**Type 2 diabetes: choose by comorbidity (ADA 2026) [C47]**

| Patient has… | Include… |
| --- | --- |
| Heart/vessel disease or high risk | GLP-1 RA and/or SGLT2i |
| Heart failure | SGLT2i |
| CKD | SGLT2i or GLP-1 RA |
| eGFR <30 | GLP-1 RA preferred |

**Checklist**

- [ ] **ADA 2026:** pick drugs by comorbidities, not A1C alone. Consider **combination therapy** at the start. [C47]
- [ ] **Metformin** is still a common first choice (usable at eGFR ≥30). [C47]
- [ ] **Screen** adults **35–70** with overweight or obesity. [C48]
- [ ] **DKA = all 3:** glucose ≥200 (or known diabetes) + ketones + acidosis (pH <7.3 or bicarbonate <18). [C49]
- [ ] **DKA potassium rule:** if K⁺ <3.5, **replace potassium before insulin.** [C49]
- [ ] **Hypothyroidism:** levothyroxine is the treatment of choice. [C50]

> **Important to remember**
> - **Trap:** "metformin only" for a patient with heart failure or CKD. **Add an SGLT2i/GLP-1 RA.** [C47]
> - **Never start insulin with K⁺ below 3.5.** [C49]
