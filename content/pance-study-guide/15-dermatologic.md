# Dermatologic (4%)

**Top 5 high-yield**

1. Melanoma ABCDEs and biopsy
2. Cellulitis vs abscess
3. Psoriasis vs eczema
4. Burns
5. Drug rashes (SJS)

**Checklist**

- [ ] **Suspicious mole:** narrow **excisional biopsy** with 1–3 mm margins (AAD 2019). [C56]
- [ ] **Abscess → I&D.** Cellulitis → antibiotics covering strep. [C35]

**Look-alike: scaly rash (classic teaching points)**

| Feature | Psoriasis | Eczema |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where | Elbows, knees, scalp | Flexures (inside elbows, knees) |
| Look | Silvery plaques | Dry, itchy patches |

> **Important to remember:** **Melanoma ABCDE** = Asymmetry, Border, Color, Diameter, Evolving.
