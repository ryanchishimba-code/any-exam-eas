# Reproductive (7%)

**Top 5 high-yield**

1. Preeclampsia criteria
2. Ectopic pregnancy: who gets methotrexate
3. Cervical screening intervals
4. Breast screening
5. Bleeding in late pregnancy

**Abbreviations:** hrHPV = high-risk HPV test. hCG = pregnancy hormone. TVUS = transvaginal ultrasound.

**Cervical screening (USPSTF) [C42]**

| Age | Options |
| --- | --- |
| 21–29 | Pap (cytology) every 3 years |
| 30–65 | Pap every 3 years, **or** hrHPV every 5 years, **or** both every 5 years |

**Checklist**

- [ ] **Breast:** mammogram every 2 years, ages **40–74**. [C43]
- [ ] **Preeclampsia:** BP **≥140/90** twice, 4 hours apart, **after 20 weeks**, *plus* protein in urine **or** a severe feature. [C44]
- [ ] **Severe features include:** BP ≥160/110, platelets <100,000, liver or kidney injury, lung fluid, new headache, vision changes. [C44]
- [ ] **Severe features → magnesium sulfate** to prevent seizures. [C44]
- [ ] **Ectopic:** diagnose with pregnancy test + **TVUS**. hCG alone can't diagnose it. [C45]
- [ ] **Methotrexate** only if **stable** with an **unruptured** mass. [C45]

**Look-alike: bleeding in late pregnancy (classic teaching points)**

| Disease | Pain? | Classic clue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Placenta previa | **Painless** | Bright red bleeding |
| Placental abruption | **Painful** | Firm, tender uterus |

> **Important to remember:** **Unstable ectopic = surgery, not methotrexate.** [C45]
