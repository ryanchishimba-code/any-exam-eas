# Reproductive (7%)

**Top 5 high-yield**

4. Breast screening

**Abbreviations:** hrHPV = high-risk HPV test. hCG = pregnancy hormone. TVUS = transvaginal ultrasound.

**Checklist**

- [ ] **Breast cancer screening:** mammogram every 2 years, ages **40–74**. ([USPSTF](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/breast-cancer-screening))

**Look-alike: bleeding in late pregnancy (classic teaching points)**

| Disease | Pain? | Classic clue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Placenta previa | **Painless** | Bright red bleeding |
| Placental abruption | **Painful** | Firm, tender uterus |
