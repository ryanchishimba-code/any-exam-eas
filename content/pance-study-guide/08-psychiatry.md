# Psychiatry / Behavioral (7%)

**Top 5 high-yield**

1. Who to screen for depression
2. Who to screen for anxiety
3. First-line depression treatment
4. Bipolar vs depression
5. Drug side-effect emergencies

**Abbreviations:** MDD = major depressive disorder. SSRI = selective serotonin reuptake inhibitor. SNRI = serotonin-norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor.

**Checklist**

- [ ] **Depression screening:** all adults (including pregnant and postpartum) and teens **12–18**. [C39]
- [ ] **Anxiety screening:** adults **≤64** and youth **8–18**. [C40]
- [ ] **MDD first treatment:** psychotherapy **or** a second-generation antidepressant (e.g., SSRI, SNRI, bupropion, mirtazapine). [C41]
- [ ] **No single antidepressant is "best" first.** Choose by side effects and patient preference. [C41]

> **Important to remember**
> - **Trap:** "SSRI is always the only first-line." Guidelines list psychotherapy *or* several drug classes. [C41]
> - Always check for **suicide risk** in a depression stem.
