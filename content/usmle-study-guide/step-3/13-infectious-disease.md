# Outpatient infection and the return visit

## Framework

Choose the drug, the duration you can defend, and the follow-up that proves it worked. The Step 2 chapter has the first dose. This chapter is stewardship and the patient who is not better.

| Problem | The plan |
| --- | --- |
| Viral upper respiratory infection | No antibiotic. Say what to come back for. |
| Uncomplicated cystitis | The short IDSA regimen (nitrofurantoin, trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole, or fosfomycin when resistance allows). Not a fluoroquinolone reflex. |
| Asymptomatic bacteriuria | Do not treat, except pregnancy and selected urologic procedures. |
| Cellulitis, no pus, going home | A drug that covers streptococci. Mark the border. Come back if it spreads. |
| Community pneumonia, low severity | The healthy-outpatient IDSA/ATS options. Follow up if they are not better. Do not stack a second antibiotic "to be sure" in a well adult. |
| Not better in 48–72 hours | Wrong bug, wrong drug, complication, or not an infection. Do not refill the same prescription as the whole answer. |
| HIV, established | Continue therapy. Do not stop for a routine cold. Opportunistic prophylaxis still follows the CD4. |
| Tick illness in the right season and region | Doxycycline when the disease is the one doxycycline treats. Do not wait on a send-out titer to start if the patient is sick with a compatible disease. |

Isolation is an order. Cough plus risk for tuberculosis is airborne, before the ward. C. difficile is contact. You already know the drug (oral vancomycin or fidaxomicin). On Step 3, also stop the inciting antibiotic if you can.

## Checklist

- [ ] No antibiotic for a virus, and say so.
- [ ] Do not treat asymptomatic bacteriuria.
- [ ] A patient who is worse needs a new thought, not a refill.
- [ ] Isolation is an order.
- [ ] Duration should be the short effective one, not a habit of two weeks.

## Common traps

- A fluoroquinolone for simple cystitis.
- Antibiotics for bronchitis.
- Stopping antiretroviral therapy for a week of sinus symptoms.
