# Women's health and prenatal follow-up

## Framework

The acute obstetric emergencies are in the Step 2 book. Step 3 is the prenatal schedule you must not skip, contraception that fits a comorbidity, and the postpartum visit.

Prenatal orders (ACOG timing): first-trimester labs including blood type, antibody screen, HIV, syphilis, hepatitis B, and a urine culture. Diabetes screen at 24 to 28 weeks. Anti-D immune globulin around 28 weeks if Rh-negative and unsensitized. Group B culture at 36 to 37 weeks. Tdap each pregnancy at 27 to 36 weeks. Preeclampsia is still the blood-pressure disease from the Step 2 chapter: magnesium for seizures, a pressure drug for severe pressure, delivery as the cure, no ACE inhibitor.

**Contraception by the condition**

| Condition | Prefer | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Migraine with aura | A non-estrogen method | Combined estrogen |
| Clot history | A non-estrogen method | Combined estrogen |
| Hypertension, older, smoking | Think hard before estrogen | Combined pills are a common wrong answer |
| Postpartum, breastfeeding, early | Progestin or nonhormonal | Estrogen too soon. Confirm the current MEC interval. |
| Needs something that survives a chaotic week | IUD or implant | A pill they will not take |

Postpartum visit: mood (depression is common and screened), bleeding, contraception, and blood pressure if the pregnancy was hypertensive. A fever and a tender uterus is endometritis, not "normal blues."

Breast screening remains the USPSTF mammogram every 2 years from 40 through 74. A lump is diagnostic imaging. ([USPSTF](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/breast-cancer-screening))

## Checklist

- [ ] Write the gestational-age order, not a generic "prenatal labs."
- [ ] No estrogen in migraine with aura or in a clot history.
- [ ] No ACE inhibitor in pregnancy.
- [ ] Postpartum fever is an infection until you have examined the patient.
- [ ] A lump is not a screening mammogram.

## Common traps

- Combined oral contraceptives for migraine with aura.
- Missing group B culture because the anatomy scan was normal.
- Calling postpartum depression "adjustment" when the screen is positive and there is no follow-up.
