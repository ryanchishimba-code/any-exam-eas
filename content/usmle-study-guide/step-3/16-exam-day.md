# Exam day

## The day

Step 3 is two days. Confirm the schedule, identification, and what Day 2's cases require on the current USMLE and FSMB pages. This chapter does not invent a start time or a case count.

**Day 1**

Read the study design before the conclusion. If the stem is an advertisement, ask what it was compared with.

**Day 2**

Orders, clock, recheck, counsel, disposition. A correct diagnosis without a follow-up is unfinished. Use the sequence in the CCS chapter. Do not try to learn the interface from a blog the morning of the exam. Use the official tutorial.

Sleep both nights. Do not add a new guideline PDF between the days.

## Common traps

- Staying up between Day 1 and Day 2 to reread heart-failure trials.
- Ending a practice case on the diagnosis and calling that CCS practice.
- Looking up items on a phone if the bulletin forbids it.
