# FAQ

## Answers

**Are you the NBME or the FSMB?**

No. This is an independent study aid.

**Will these orders match the real CCS software?**

They are the clinical sequence. This book is not the Primum interface and does not claim to reproduce its scoring.

**Where is the passing score?**

On the official score-reporting page. It is not in this book, on purpose.

**How is this different from Step 2?**

Step 2 is the next branch. Step 3 is the sequence, the follow-up, the disposition, and the paper or the ad.

**How is this different from the feed?**

The feed is one fact. The book is the order you place those facts in.

**What if a cutoff changed?**

Follow the linked guideline. CLINICAL-REVIEW.md is the list a physician should recheck before anyone relies on a number.

## Common traps

- Using a chapter as an order set on a ward.
- Quoting a practice-case result as a Step 3 score.
- Skipping Day 1 biostatistics because Day 2 feels more clinical.
