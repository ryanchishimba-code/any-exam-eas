# Step 3 questions people ask

> **Study aid only.** The specifications and the Bulletin win. This book does not promise a pass, a score, or a CCS point total.

## What this chapter covers

What this Step adds after Step 2 CK, how the two days differ, and how this book, the bank, and the feed divide the work.

## The framework

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| What is Step 3 for? | Unsupervised general medicine. The USMLE text is explicit that you are a generalist on this exam even if your residency is not. |
| Who runs it? | The USMLE program, which belongs to the FSMB and the NBME. Step 3 registration is tied to the FSMB. AnyExamEasy is not either of them. |
| How is it not Step 2? | Two days. A larger biostatistics share. Continuing care and disposition. CCS. Day 1 still asks for science and ethics. |
| Does CCS decide the result? | It affects the result and can affect pass or fail. The Bulletin says its contribution is no greater than its share of time. Anyone who gives you a percent beyond that is inventing it. |
| How many cases, how long? | 13 to 14 cases, each at most 10 or 20 minutes of real time, on Day 2. Confirm before you test. |
| Do I need the Step 1 book? | For a mechanism you have lost, yes. Do not restudy it cover to cover. |
| Do I need the Step 2 book? | When the miss was the disease or the acute guideline. Stay here when the miss was disposition, follow-up, or the clock. |
| What about the feed? | One fact, after the chapter. Useless during a CCS case. Dangerous if the card’s age cutoff contradicts USPSTF or CDC. |
| Can I sit Step 3 before Step 2 CK? | No. The Bulletin requires Step 1 and Step 2 CK passed. |
| Will a year of residency make this easy? | USMLE says that year should help. It does not say it is sufficient. People fail this exam after a clinical year. Respect the biostatistics and the CCS interface. |
| Is a passing score printed in this book? | No. Look up the current standard. It changes. |

## Checklist

- [ ] You can describe Day 1 and Day 2 in two sentences each.
- [ ] You have no invented CCS percentage in your notes.
- [ ] You know which book to open for a given kind of miss.
- [ ] You have used the official CCS software.
- [ ] You can name the FSMB and the NBME as the owners.

## Common traps

- “I see patients, so I do not need to study.” The frames include initial workup and urgent care, plus papers, which your clinic may not drill.
- Shotgun CCS orders. They are not how you practice and they are not how this book tells you to practice.
- Treating AnyExamEasy as if it were the USMLE program. It is not, and it is not endorsed by the NBME or the FSMB.

## Practice this in the Qbank

Readiness is a mixed Day 1 block you have not seen and a CCS case you have not memorized. If those are orderly, you are using the method. A percent on either one is not a promise about the other, or about the real day.
