# How Step 3 works

## The outline

Step 3 is two days. Day 1 is Foundations of Independent Practice. Day 2 is Advanced Clinical Medicine and includes CCS. The score is numeric. This book does not state a passing score, a mean, a case count, or a minute count. The FSMB and USMLE bulletin are the source of truth.

Recorded ranges from the content outline on 2026-09-06. The live page wins if they have changed.

| Task | Recorded range |
| --- | --- |
| Diagnosis | 33–36% |
| Foundational science | 11–12% |
| Practice-based learning | 11–13% |
| Pharmacotherapy | 9–13% |
| Laboratory and diagnostic studies | 9–12% |
| Prognosis | 8–11% |
| Health maintenance | 6–11% |
| Mixed management | 6–11% |
| History and physical | 5–9% |
| Clinical interventions | 5–9% |
| Communication and systems | 7–9% |

Biostatistics and population health are recorded at about 6–12%, higher than on Step 1. That is why they have their own chapter.

**What Day 2 adds**

You are not finished when you name the disease. You place orders, advance time, recheck, counsel, prevent, and choose a disposition. A correct diagnosis with no monitoring is an incomplete case.

## Common traps

- Ignoring prognosis items. They are their own range.
- Treating CCS as "type the diagnosis." The interface wants orders.
- Quoting a forum's case count as if it were the bulletin.
