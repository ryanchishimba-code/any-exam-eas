# Pulmonary and the short stay

## Framework

Decide the bed, the oxygen target, and the follow-up. The Step 2 chapter has the acute branch. This one is what you do after the first hour and what you send the patient home with.

| Situation | Orders and the clock |
| --- | --- |
| Asthma, going home | Inhaled corticosteroid-formoterol per the GINA track, a written plan, and follow-up. Do not send albuterol alone. Recheck that they can walk and talk. |
| COPD exacerbation, improving | Steroid course, antibiotic only when the sputum and the dyspnea say so, oxygen target about 88–92% if they retain carbon dioxide, and inhalers that match GOLD group before discharge. Smoking cessation treatment. |
| Pneumonia, admitting | Severity decides the floor versus home. Antibiotics that match the ward, cultures if you are admitting, and a follow-up chest film only when one is indicated (smoker, incomplete recovery), not as a ritual the next morning for every young adult. |
| Suspected tuberculosis | Airborne isolation before the ward transfer. Do not "rule out later." |
| Possible pulmonary embolism, stable enough for a ward | Anticoagulation while you confirm, if bleeding risk allows. A home plan needs a reliable patient and a drug they can get tonight. |

Prognosis language the exam likes: a patient who cannot protect the airway or who is tiring is not a floor patient. Say intensive care. A patient on room air, eating, and improving can leave when follow-up exists.

## Checklist

- [ ] Do not discharge on a short-acting beta-agonist alone.
- [ ] Name the oxygen target in a retainer.
- [ ] Isolate before you move a possible tuberculosis case.
- [ ] Floor versus intensive care is a breathing decision.
- [ ] Home inhalers should match the group, not the hospital's default nebulizer.

## Common traps

- Stopping chronic oxygen because today's saturation is 94% on the cannula.
- A macrolide for a ward pneumonia in a patient with comorbidities, copied from the healthy-outpatient rule.
- Sending possible tuberculosis to a shared room while sputum is pending.
