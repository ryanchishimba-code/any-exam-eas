# How to use the Step 3 book

**Study aid only.** Not medical advice. Not a promise of a result. Independent of the USMLE program, the NBME, and the FSMB.

Step 3 is two days. Day 1 leans on foundations of independent practice: biostatistics, ethics, and science applied to a decision. Day 2 leans on advanced clinical medicine, including computer-based case simulations. This book does not include a CCS simulator. It teaches the order of orders. The question bank's CCS-style items are practice, not the real interface.

The feed cards are single facts. Do not reread them as a substitute for a sequence.

## Study plan

| Plan | Shape |
| --- | --- |
| 2 weeks | Day-1 skills (biostatistics, ethics, abstracts) on days 1–3. Then the four heaviest management systems. One CCS-style sequence every day. |
| 6 weeks | Two systems a week, plus an abstract or an ad twice a week. End each system by writing the orders you would place, including counseling. |
| 12 weeks | One system a week. Keep a running list of "orders I forget": pregnancy test, glucose, isolation, vaccines, follow-up. |

Give biostatistics and prevention more time than you did for Step 1. The recorded outline weights them higher here.

## Common traps

- Studying Step 2 "next test" and never practicing a sequence of orders.
- Treating a pharmaceutical ad as a journal club you must believe.
- Promising yourself a score because a checklist felt complete.
