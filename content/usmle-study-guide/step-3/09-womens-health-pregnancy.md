# Women's health and pregnancy

> **Study aid only.** ACOG for pregnancy. CDC Medical Eligibility Criteria for contraception. USPSTF for the screening ages, which differ from some specialty advice. Pregnancy and the female reproductive system are about 7–9% of Step 3 MCQs. CCS cases include prenatal visits and bleeding.

## What this chapter covers

The prenatal visit, the hypertensive disorders, contraception in a generalist clinic, and the gynecologic problems you either finish or send today. The Step 2 obstetrics chapter holds the emergency detail. This one holds the independent-practice visit.

## The framework

A prenatal visit is blood pressure, symptoms of preeclampsia (headache, visual change, right-upper-quadrant pain, swelling that is sudden), fetal movement when it is time, and the lab that is due. Severe features mean the hospital and magnesium, not a repeat pressure tomorrow. Magnesium toxicity is lost reflexes then respiratory depression. Calcium is the antidote.

| Visit | Finish it yourself or move the person |
| --- | --- |
| Contraception, migraine with aura, or clot history, or smoking over 35 | No estrogen. The CDC chart is the authority. A long-acting method is still available. |
| Prenatal labs | Type and antibody, hemoglobin, HIV, syphilis, hepatitis B, rubella. Treat HIV and syphilis. Do not calendar them for after delivery. |
| Rh-negative | Anti-D at the routine time and after bleeding. |
| GBS | Late-pregnancy screen and intrapartum treatment as ACOG directs. |
| Postpartum check | Mood, bleeding, blood pressure, contraception, and the vaccine that was missed. Preeclampsia can present after birth. |
| Postmenopausal bleeding | Cancer until an endometrial assessment says otherwise. Not reassurance. |
| Breast mass | Imaging that matches age. A negative mammogram does not erase a palpable mass. |
| Screening mammogram | USPSTF: offer every 2 years from 40 to 74. |
| Cervical screen | Start at 21. Interval by age and test. |
| First-trimester bleeding, unstable or pain with dizziness | Ectopic pathway. Emergency. |
| Third-trimester bleeding | Previa until the ultrasound says no. No digital exam. Abruption if painful. Hospital. |

Gestational diabetes screening follows ACOG. Do not mix one-step and two-step cutoffs. Blood pressure treatment in pregnancy is not the adult PREVENT threshold. Use ACOG’s thresholds. The 2025 adult guideline does not run the prenatal clinic.

Medication abortion and emergency contraception are different interventions. Do not describe one as the other. Follow the current FDA label and your jurisdiction’s law. This book is not a legal guide and not a protocol.

## Checklist

- [ ] Preeclampsia symptoms are asked, not assumed absent.
- [ ] Syphilis and HIV in pregnancy are treated now.
- [ ] Postmenopausal bleeding is evaluated.
- [ ] A palpable breast mass survives a negative mammogram.
- [ ] Estrogen is withheld when the CDC chart says so.

## Common traps

- Adult hypertension targets applied to a pregnant patient.
- Stopping all psychiatric medicines at the first positive pregnancy test, including one the person needs to stay well.
- Reassurance for postmenopausal bleeding because the last Pap was normal. The Pap is not an endometrial test.
- A feed card that starts cervical screening at the onset of sexual activity.

## Practice this in the Qbank

Prenatal items: gestational age plus one danger. Clinic items: screening or a contraindication. CCS prenatal cases should include the blood pressure, the symptom screen, and the lab that is due, not a shotgun of imaging. The feed can hold a contraindication to estrogen. Check it against the CDC chart.
