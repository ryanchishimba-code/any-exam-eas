# USMLE study guides — clinical review

This file is for a physician. It is not student-facing copy. AnyExamEasy is independent of the USMLE program, the NBME, and the FSMB. Nothing in the books promises a result.

The chapters are exam maps. A number below is a claim a physician should check against the live source before the books are shown to students. Outline ranges were copied from the in-repo record of the USMLE content outline dated 2026-09-06 (`src/lib/exam-prep/usmle/official-content-model.ts`). If that page has changed, the page wins.

The USMLE feed (`content/usmle-feed`) holds single facts. These chapters are frameworks. They are not a second copy of the cards.

## Every book

- No item count, no passing score, no pass rate, no mean.
- Step 1 is described as reported pass/fail. Confirm on the current bulletin.
- Step 2 CK and Step 3 are described as numeric scores without a cut point.
- Step 3 is described as two days, with CCS on Day 2, without a case count.
- Reperfusion minute targets are deliberately not stated. The text says to confirm them on the current ACS guideline.

## Step 1

Mechanism-level consensus only. No treatment protocol.

- Glycolysis committed step is PFK-1. Gluconeogenesis uses four replacement enzymes, including biotin for pyruvate carboxylase.
- G6PD assay during an acute hemolysis can be falsely normal.
- Mitochondrial transmission is maternal.
- Hypersensitivity types I–IV as tabulated.
- Autonomic receptor table and zero-order examples (phenytoin, high-dose aspirin, alcohol).
- Acetaminophen toxicity and N-acetylcysteine. Classic chemo organ toxicities (bleomycin lung, doxorubicin heart, cyclophosphamide bladder).
- Murmur timing table. Obstruction louder when the ventricle is smaller.
- A-a gradient table. Loop diuretics waste calcium. Thiazides do not.
- Normal anion-gap acidosis: diarrhea can acidify urine. Distal RTA cannot.
- Hepatitis B serology sequence as taught for exams.
- Primary versus secondary adrenal failure and pigmentation.
- Continuous GnRH suppresses. 5α-reductase versus androgen receptor.
- MCA face and arm. ACA leg. Wallenberg without hemiparesis.
- Oncogene one hit, suppressor two hits.
- Pemphigus intraepidermal, pemphigoid subepidermal.
- Shock table (cold full neck veins versus warm vasodilated).
- Sensitivity, specificity, and predictive value moving with prevalence.

## What a reviewer should do

1. Open each linked page and mark any number that has moved.
2. Replace the chapter sentence. Do not leave a stale cutoff because the table looked tidy.
3. Do not add a pass rate, a mean, or an item count when you edit.
4. The books stay hidden until `USMLE_STUDY_GUIDE_PUBLIC` or `NEXT_PUBLIC_USMLE_STUDY_GUIDE_PUBLIC` is set. Turning them on is a separate decision from this review.
