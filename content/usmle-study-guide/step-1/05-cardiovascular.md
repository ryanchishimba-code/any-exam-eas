# Cardiovascular mechanisms

## Framework

Read the heart as a pump with a circuit. Most Step 1 cardiac items are a pressure, a valve, or a channel.

**Murmur timing is physiology**

| When you hear it | What is happening | The move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Systolic, right upper sternal border, to the carotids | Flow across a tight aortic valve, or high flow | Severe symptomatic stenosis is a valve problem, not a rate problem. |
| Holosystolic at the apex to the axilla | Mitral regurgitation. The ventricle leaks backward all of systole. | Ask why the valve fails: ischemia, prolapse, dilation. |
| Diastolic rumble at the apex | Mitral stenosis. Blood struggles to enter the ventricle. | Opening snap. Think rheumatic disease on exams. |
| Wide pulse pressure, early diastolic at the left sternal border | Aortic regurgitation. The aorta empties backward. | The ventricle sees both forward and leaked volume. |

Hypertrophic obstructive cardiomyopathy gets louder when the ventricle is smaller (Valsalva, standing) and softer when the ventricle is fuller (squat, handgrip). That is the opposite of most flow murmurs.

**Ischemia**

A plaque ruptures. Platelets and thrombus close the lumen. ST elevation means a full-thickness injury current. Subendocardial ischemia can be ST depression. Troponin rises because myocytes died. The mechanism chapter stops there. The reperfusion decision is a Step 2 question.

**Channels, only the classes that change the answer**

| Class | Channel | What you use it for in a stem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | Sodium | Rhythm. Watch QRS widening as a toxicity. |
| II | Beta receptor | Slow the node. Not a rescue for a lung that needs β2. |
| III | Potassium | Prolong the action potential. QT is the toxicity. |
| IV | Calcium at the node | Slow the AV node. Do not pile this on a beta-blocker in a failing heart. |

Heart-failure physiology: forward failure is low output. Backward failure is congestion. Left-sided backup is lungs. Right-sided backup is body veins. A stiff ventricle (preserved ejection fraction) fails when it cannot fill. A weak ventricle fails when it cannot eject.

## Checklist

- [ ] Systole versus diastole before the diagnosis.
- [ ] Maneuvers that shrink the ventricle make obstruction louder.
- [ ] Left backup is pulmonary. Right backup is systemic veins.
- [ ] Class III prolongs QT. Class I widens QRS.
- [ ] Troponin means myocytes died. It does not name the artery.

## Common traps

- Calling every systolic murmur aortic stenosis. Location and radiation decide.
- Treating hypertrophic obstruction with a pure vasodilator. The obstruction gets worse.
- Using a non-dihydropyridine calcium blocker and a beta-blocker together because both "slow the rate."
