# Reproduction and development

> **Study aid only.** Embryology and reproductive physiology. Prenatal management is an ACOG topic in the Step 2 and Step 3 books.

## What this chapter covers

Sex development, the menstrual cycle as feedback, pregnancy physiology, and the embryology errors that become named congenital disease. Human development is a small explicit range (about 1–3%) and also sits inside the heavy reproductive and endocrine block.

## The framework

Gonads and internal ducts are a fork. The SRY gene on Y pushes the undifferentiated gonad toward testis. Sertoli cells make anti-Müllerian hormone, so the female internal ducts regress. Leydig cells make testosterone, so the Wolffian ducts become epididymis, vas, and seminal vesicles. Dihydrotestosterone, made by 5-alpha-reductase, virilizes the external genitalia. Without SRY, the gonad becomes an ovary, Müllerian structures persist, and external genitalia develop along female lines. A defect at each step has a different phenotype. Name the step.

| Step that failed | Internal ducts | External genitalia |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No anti-Müllerian hormone effect in an XY fetus | Both systems can persist | Male, if androgen worked |
| Androgen receptor does not work | Wolffian structures do not mature | Female or under-virilized. Testes are present. |
| No 5-alpha-reductase | Wolffian structures present | Under-virilized until puberty raises testosterone |
| Congenital adrenal hyperplasia, XX, androgen excess | Uterus present | Virilized |

The menstrual cycle is negative feedback until it isn’t. Early follicular growth is FSH. Estrogen then feeds back negatively. A sustained high estrogen switches to positive feedback, the LH surge, and ovulation. The corpus luteum makes progesterone, which quiets the endometrium into a secretory lining. No fertilization: the corpus luteum fades, progesterone falls, the lining sheds. Fertilization: hCG keeps the corpus luteum alive until the placenta takes over steroid production.

## Pregnancy physiology worth the points

Blood volume and cardiac output rise. Systemic resistance falls, so blood pressure often dips mid-pregnancy. Tidal volume rises and PCO2 falls. A “normal” nonpregnant PCO2 is a warning in pregnancy. GFR rises, so creatinine should look a little lower. The placenta is an organ of gas exchange. Fetal hemoglobin holds oxygen (left-shifted curve). Oxygenated blood prefers the ductus venosus and the foramen ovale on the way to the brain and heart.

Teratogens are mechanism plus timing. The all-or-none period is very early. Organogenesis is the vulnerable window. Learn a short list by mechanism (folate antagonism, vascular disruption, ACE inhibitor effect on fetal kidney, isotretinoin on neural crest and the face) rather than a poster of drug names. Confirm any drug you might actually prescribe against a current label. This page is not a prescribing guide.

## Embryology that explains a lesion

- Neural tube closes early. Folate deficiency raises the risk of a failure to close. The lesion level decides motor and continence findings.
- Branchial apparatus: a pouch, groove, or arch error explains a lateral neck cyst or a missing parathyroid and thymus (third pouch).
- Neural crest builds peripheral neurons, melanocytes, the adrenal medulla, and parts of the face and conotruncal cushions. A crest failure can therefore be pigment plus heart plus gut innervation.
- Vitelline duct that stays open is a Meckel diverticulum. It can contain gastric mucosa and bleed.
- Physiologic midgut herniation returns. If it does not, you have an omphalocele (covered, midline). Gastroschisis is a wall defect, usually to the right of the cord, without a sac. Do not swap them.

### Androgen-insensitivity pattern

The receptor does not hear testosterone or DHT. The testis made AMH, so the uterus is absent. Breasts develop from estrogen made out of the unused androgen.

**Vignette.** A 16-year-old girl has not menstruated. Breast development is present. Pubic hair is sparse. There is no uterus. A gonad is felt in the inguinal canal.
**Reasoning.** AMH worked, so there is no uterus. Androgen did not masculinize the outside or grow pubic hair. The inguinal gonad is a testis. The diagnosis is Androgen-insensitivity pattern.
**Pair.** Müllerian agenesis is XX, has a uterus missing, and has normal axillary and pubic hair because androgens work.
**NBME trap.** Calling every absent uterus androgen insensitivity. Hair and the karyotype split them.
**Hook.** No uterus and no pubic hair in an XY person is a deaf receptor.

### 5-alpha-reductase deficiency

Testosterone built the Wolffian ducts. DHT, which the external genitalia needed in utero, was missing. At puberty the testosterone surge is enough to virilize.

**Vignette.** A child raised as a girl grows a phallus and a deep voice at 13. There is no menstruation. Gonads are in the labia.
**Reasoning.** The external genitalia were under-virilized at birth because DHT was missing, and the puberty surge of testosterone finished the job. The diagnosis is 5-alpha-reductase deficiency.
**Pair.** Androgen insensitivity never answers testosterone. This enzyme defect answers testosterone and fails only the DHT step.
**NBME trap.** Calling it a late-onset adrenal hyperplasia. The karyotype and the internal ducts are male.
**Hook.** Internal male, external female, then a male puberty.

### 21-hydroxylase deficiency

Cortisol and aldosterone both need 21-hydroxylase. Substrate spills into androgens. Salt is lost.

**Vignette.** A two-week-old with ambiguous genitalia is in shock. Sodium is low. Potassium is high. 17-hydroxyprogesterone is high.
**Reasoning.** The common adrenal enzyme block wastes salt and overproduces androgen. A high 17-hydroxyprogesterone is the backed-up substrate. The diagnosis is 21-hydroxylase deficiency.
**Pair.** 11-hydroxylase also virilizes an XX infant but holds salt with a mineralocorticoid precursor, so the pressure is high.
**NBME trap.** Giving the baby more androgen attention and missing the potassium. The crisis is the salt.
**Hook.** Virilized and salt-wasting is 21. Virilized and hypertensive is 11.

### Müllerian agenesis

The Müllerian ducts did not form. Ovaries and androgens are fine, so breasts and hair are normal.

**Vignette.** A 17-year-old has breast development, normal pubic hair, cyclic pelvic pain is absent, and imaging shows no uterus. Ovaries are present.
**Reasoning.** Estrogen and androgen targets work. Only the uterine primordium failed. The diagnosis is Müllerian agenesis.
**Pair.** Androgen insensitivity has sparse hair and an XY karyotype.
**NBME trap.** Operating as if a transverse septum is the only possibility before imaging the kidneys, which share a developmental field.
**Hook.** Normal hair, no uterus, XX.

### Polycystic ovary physiology

The ovary makes extra androgen. LH is tonically high. Insulin resistance often sits underneath and makes the ovary worse. This is a Step 1 mechanism. The diagnostic criteria and the metformin question are later books.

**Vignette.** A 24-year-old has months between periods, hair on the chin, and a BMI of 34. LH is higher than FSH. Glucose is borderline.
**Reasoning.** Tonic LH drives theca androgen. Insulin resistance amplifies it. The cycle never gets a clean ovulatory progesterone peak. The diagnosis is Polycystic ovary physiology.
**Pair.** Cushing syndrome has a high cortisol, striae, and a different fat pattern. Ovarian androgen excess does not.
**NBME trap.** Calling every hairy woman a testosterone-secreting tumor. Tumors are usually higher androgens and a faster course.
**Hook.** Irregular cycles plus androgen plus a metabolic backdrop.

### The menstrual cycle as feedback

Early estrogen is negative feedback. Sustained high estrogen flips to positive feedback and the LH surge. The corpus luteum makes progesterone. No pregnancy, progesterone falls, the lining sheds. hCG keeps the corpus luteum alive if a blastocyst implants.

**Vignette.** Serum estrogen has been climbing for two days. LH then spikes. A dominant follicle is seen.
**Reasoning.** The pituitary flipped from restraint to a surge because estrogen stayed high. Ovulation follows the surge, not the other way around. The diagnosis is The menstrual cycle as feedback.
**Pair.** The follicular phase is estrogen. The luteal phase is progesterone. Bleeding is progesterone withdrawal.
**NBME trap.** Saying FSH causes ovulation. The surge that ovulates is LH.
**Hook.** Estrogen builds. LH releases. Progesterone holds.

### Endometriosis

Endometrial tissue responds to the cycle wherever it sits, including the ovary (a chocolate cyst) and the peritoneum. Bleeding where there is no exit causes pain and adhesions.

**Vignette.** A 30-year-old has painful periods that have worsened, pain with intercourse, and an ovary with a cyst full of old blood.
**Reasoning.** Ectopic endometrium bled into a closed space. The cyst is old blood. The diagnosis is not an infection because the pain is cyclic and the fever is absent. The diagnosis is Endometriosis.
**Pair.** Adenomyosis lives in the muscle and the uterus is bulky. Endometriosis lives outside and the uterus may be fixed.
**NBME trap.** Treating cyclic pain as pelvic inflammatory disease without fever or a risk story.
**Hook.** Chocolate cyst, cyclic pain, glands where they should not be.

### Leiomyoma versus endometrial cancer

A leiomyoma is a benign smooth-muscle tumor, estrogen-sensitive, whorled. Postmenopausal bleeding is cancer until the endometrium is sampled. A fibroid does not excuse skipping that sample after menopause.

**Vignette.** A 62-year-old has spotting. The uterus is slightly enlarged. She is not on estrogen.
**Reasoning.** Bleeding after menopause is an endometrial evaluation, not reassurance. A fibroid can coexist and is not the answer until the lining is benign. The diagnosis is Leiomyoma versus endometrial cancer.
**Pair.** A fibroid is a muscle whorl. Endometrial carcinoma is a glandular lining in an older, often obese, unopposed-estrogen host.
**NBME trap.** Ultrasound that says fibroid, and nobody biopsies the lining.
**Hook.** After menopause, bleeding is the lining until proven otherwise.

### Testicular torsion versus epididymitis

The cord twisted. Ischemia is measured in hours. Epididymitis is usually older or sexually active, with fever and a gradual onset, and the testis itself is not ischemic at the start.

**Vignette.** A 15-year-old wakes with severe left scrotal pain and vomiting. The testis sits high. Lifting it does not relieve the pain.
**Reasoning.** Sudden pain, a high testis, and vomiting are ischemia. Imaging is optional when the exam is this clear. The later books say go to the operating room. The diagnosis is Testicular torsion versus epididymitis.
**Pair.** Epididymitis has fever, pyuria, and a gradual course. The cremasteric reflex is often present.
**NBME trap.** Antibiotics for a torsion because the patient is a teenager.
**Hook.** Sudden, high, nauseated: untwist in the operating room.

### Varicocele and the left vein

The left gonadal vein drains to the renal vein at a right angle. A sudden right-sided or a non-decompressing varicocele is a mass until imaged.

**Vignette.** A man has a left scrotal swelling that appears when he stands and disappears when he lies down. It feels like vessels.
**Reasoning.** Venous valves and the left-sided drainage failed. The diagnosis is a varicocele. A new right-sided one that does not empty needs a look for a retroperitoneal block. The diagnosis is Varicocele and the left vein.
**Pair.** Hydrocele transilluminates and is fluid around the testis. Varicocele is vessels and position-dependent.
**NBME trap.** Reassurance for a right-sided varicocele that stays full when supine.
**Hook.** Left and positional is a vein. Right and fixed is a mass.

### Prostate, the zones

Cancer starts in the peripheral zone and is felt on a rectal exam. Benign hyperplasia is the transition zone and squeezes the urethra. They are different lesions that can coexist.

**Vignette.** An older man has a hard nodule on the periphery of the prostate. Urinary flow is still reasonable. PSA is discussed as a shared decision, not a mandate.
**Reasoning.** The periphery is where adenocarcinoma starts. Obstruction is the central zone. A hard peripheral nodule is cancer until biopsied. The diagnosis is Prostate, the zones.
**Pair.** BPH is central and obstructive. Cancer is peripheral and nodular.
**NBME trap.** A normal flow rate used to clear a hard nodule.
**Hook.** Periphery is cancer. Center is the stream.

### Spermatogenesis needs coolness and FSH and testosterone

FSH and Sertoli cells run spermatogenesis. LH and Leydig cells make testosterone. Kallmann is GnRH neurons that never migrated, so smell and puberty both fail. Klinefelter is XXY, tall, small firm testes, and a high FSH.

**Vignette.** A tall man has small firm testes, gynecomastia, and azoospermia. The karyotype has an extra X.
**Reasoning.** An extra X dysgeneses the tubules. Testosterone can be low-normal and FSH is high because the tubules do not provide feedback. The diagnosis is Spermatogenesis needs coolness and FSH and testosterone.
**Pair.** Kallmann has no smell and low GnRH, so FSH and LH are low. Klinefelter has high FSH.
**NBME trap.** Giving testosterone and expecting sperm. Testosterone does not rebuild destroyed tubules.
**Hook.** XXY is high FSH and firm small testes. No smell and low FSH is Kallmann.

### Placental hormone switch

hCG keeps the corpus luteum alive until the placenta can make progesterone. A molar pregnancy is trophoblast without a viable fetus, a very high hCG, and a risk of a later malignancy that is followed, not assumed.

**Vignette.** A woman has severe vomiting, a uterus larger than dates, a very high hCG, and an ultrasound with vesicles and no fetus.
**Reasoning.** Trophoblast overgrew. The diagnosis is a hydatidiform mole. The trap is ordinary hyperemesis, which does not have a snowstorm uterus. The diagnosis is Placental hormone switch.
**Pair.** A complete mole is usually empty of fetal tissue. A partial mole may have fetal parts and a triploid story. Both are followed for hCG.
**NBME trap.** Stopping follow-up of hCG because the uterus was emptied.
**Hook.** Snowstorm, huge hCG, no fetus.

### Teratogens by the step they break

Organogenesis is the vulnerable window. Before that, injury is often all or nothing. After that, growth and function can still be hurt. This page is not a prescribing guide. The label and ACOG win for a real patient.

**Vignette.** A fetus exposed in the first trimester to a vitamin A derivative has abnormal ears and a heart defect.
**Reasoning.** Neural crest and branchial development were hit during organogenesis. The diagnosis is isotretinoin embryopathy. The trap is blaming a drug taken in the third trimester for a missing structure. The diagnosis is Teratogens by the step they break.
**Pair.** ACE inhibitors hurt fetal kidneys later, when urine production matters. Isotretinoin hits early patterning.
**NBME trap.** Memorizing a poster instead of the trimester and the structure.
**Hook.** Timing plus the structure the drug is famous for.

## Stem craft

Differentiation and the cycle, without the syndrome name in the stem.

The vignette withholds the name. The reasoning earns it.

**Vignette 1.** An XY infant has a uterus and a testis. External genitalia are male.
**Reasoning.** Anti-Müllerian hormone did not act, so the Müllerian duct stayed. Androgen worked, so the outside is male. The trap is androgen insensitivity, which removes the uterus and under-virilizes the outside.

**Vignette 2.** Sustained estrogen for two days, then an LH spike, then a rise in progesterone.
**Reasoning.** Positive feedback caused the surge. Progesterone means a corpus luteum formed. The trap is saying FSH ovulates the follicle.

**Vignette 3.** A woman misses a period. A hormone that shares the LH receptor is rising. The corpus luteum does not fade.
**Reasoning.** hCG from a trophoblast. The trap is a pituitary LH tumor. The calendar of a missed period is pregnancy until proven otherwise.

**Vignette 4.** Painful periods, a fixed uterus, and an ovarian cyst full of old blood. The uterine cavity itself looks normal.
**Reasoning.** Endometriosis. Adenomyosis would enlarge the muscle. Infection would bring fever. The trap is calling every pelvic pain pelvic inflammatory disease.

**Vignette 5.** A hard peripheral prostate nodule. The urinary stream is still fine.
**Reasoning.** Cancer starts in the periphery. Obstruction is the transition zone. A good stream does not clear a hard nodule.

## Another pass on the stems

More differentiation stems.

The vignette withholds the name. The reasoning earns it.

**Vignette 1.** An XY newborn has a uterus and male external genitalia.
**Reasoning.** Anti-Müllerian hormone failed and androgen worked. Androgen insensitivity would not leave a uterus and would not virilize the outside. The trap is one intersex label.

**Vignette 2.** An XX infant is virilized and hypertensive. Potassium is not high.
**Reasoning.** 11-hydroxylase deficiency. A mineralocorticoid precursor holds salt. 21-hydroxylase wastes salt and raises potassium. The blood pressure is the split.

**Vignette 3.** Breast development is present, pubic hair is normal, there is no uterus, and the karyotype is XX.
**Reasoning.** Müllerian agenesis. Androgen insensitivity is XY and has sparse hair. The trap is one absent-uterus pathway.

**Vignette 4.** A painless testicular mass in a 28-year-old. It does not transilluminate.
**Reasoning.** Germ-cell tumor until proven otherwise. A hydrocele transilluminates. Epididymitis hurts and has fever. The trap is antibiotics for a painless solid mass.

## Checklist

- [ ] Name the sexual-differentiation step, not the syndrome first.
- [ ] LH surge needs sustained estrogen. Progesterone is the luteal hormone.
- [ ] Pregnancy lowers PCO2 and the expected creatinine.
- [ ] Timing of a teratogen matters as much as the drug.
- [ ] Omphalocele is covered. Gastroschisis is not.

## Common traps

- Calling every virilized XX newborn “androgen exposure” without asking whether a uterus is present. The uterus tells you the gonadal and Müllerian story.
- Treating hCG and LH as unrelated. hCG can stimulate the LH receptor. That is why it maintains the corpus luteum, and why some tumors cause hormone effects.
- Memorizing week numbers you cannot defend. If an item needs a week, it usually also gives a clue. Confirm standard obstetric weeks in the ACOG-based chapters rather than inventing them here.
- Studying development as trivia disconnected from the adult disease it becomes.
