# Hematology and oncology mechanisms

## Framework

Anemia is production, destruction, or loss. Coagulation is which factor is missing and whether the test looks at that arm. Cancer is a gene that should have stopped, or one that will not.

**Anemia by mechanism**

| Clue | Mechanism |
| --- | --- |
| Low reticulocytes | The marrow is not answering. Iron, B12, folate, aplasia, infiltration. |
| High reticulocytes | Loss or destruction. The marrow is trying. |
| Small cells | Iron deficiency, thalassemia, anemia of chronic disease sometimes. |
| Big cells | B12, folate, and some drugs that block DNA. |
| Bite cells, Heinz bodies | Oxidant injury. G6PD. |
| Schistocytes | Mechanical destruction. Microangiopathy. |

Iron deficiency is low ferritin. Anemia of chronic disease traps iron, so ferritin is normal or high and the iron is low. Thalassemia trait makes very small cells with a red-cell count that is not as low as the hemoglobin suggests. Do not give iron for that.

**Coagulation**

PT looks at the extrinsic arm (factor VII and the common path). PTT looks at the intrinsic arm (XII, XI, IX, VIII) and the common path. Mixing with normal plasma corrects a missing factor and does not correct an inhibitor. Hemophilia A is VIII, hemophilia B is IX. Both are X-linked and bleed into joints. von Willebrand disease is the most common inherited bleeding disease on exams: long PTT sometimes, long bleeding time, mucosal bleeding, because the platelet cannot stick and factor VIII is less stable.

**Cancer genes, the pairs worth knowing**

A proto-oncogene needs one bad copy to push (RAS, MYC, BCR-ABL). A tumor suppressor needs both copies lost (RB, TP53, APC). BCR-ABL is the Philadelphia chromosome, a tyrosine kinase, and the reason a kinase inhibitor exists. Chronic myeloid leukemia is the classic stem. Do not memorize every translocation. Know the one the stem describes.

## Checklist

- [ ] Reticulocytes before the vitamin.
- [ ] Ferritin separates iron deficiency from anemia of chronic disease.
- [ ] Mixing study: correction means a missing factor.
- [ ] Oncogene is one hit. Suppressor is two hits.
- [ ] Schistocytes are mechanical, not an iron problem.

## Common traps

- Pouring iron into thalassemia trait.
- Calling a long PTT that corrects on mixing an inhibitor. Correction means deficiency.
- Treating BCR-ABL as a tumor suppressor. It is a kinase that is on.
