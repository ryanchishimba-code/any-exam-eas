**Five sentences worth being able to say**

- PFK-1 commits glycolysis. Gluconeogenesis replaces only the irreversible steps.
- Sensitivity is about the people with disease. Predictive value cares how rare the disease is.
- Competitive antagonism shifts the curve and keeps the maximum.
- Left heart backup is the lung. Right heart backup is the veins.
- A capable adult decides, including a refusal.

## Common traps

- Using this page as the whole book the night before.
- Memorizing the table of chapters and skipping the mechanism inside them.
