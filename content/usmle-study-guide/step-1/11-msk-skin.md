# Musculoskeletal, skin, and rheumatology

## Framework

The outline gives this system a real share. The exam wants the layer, the antibody that matches the disease, and the bone tumor's age.

**Antibody is a diagnosis only when the stem already fits**

| Disease | The antibody the stem pairs with it | The mechanism clue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rheumatoid arthritis | Rheumatoid factor, anti-CCP | Synovium. Morning stiffness. Spares the DIP on classic items. |
| SLE | Anti-dsDNA, anti-Smith | Immune complexes. dsDNA tracks kidney disease on exams. |
| Diffuse systemic sclerosis | Anti-Scl-70 | Skin and lung fibrosis. |
| Limited systemic sclerosis | Anticentromere | CREST pattern. Pulmonary hypertension. |
| Drug lupus | Antihistone | Hydralazine, procainamide, isoniazid are the classic drugs. |
| Myositis | Anti-Jo-1 when the lung is also involved | Proximal weakness, not pain as the main feature. |

**Bone, by age**

| Age and site | The tumor exams mean |
| --- | --- |
| Teen, metaphysis, sunburst or Codman triangle | Osteosarcoma. |
| Teen, diaphysis, onion skin | Ewing. |
| Adult, epiphysis | Giant cell. |
| Older adult, new pain, punched-out or blastic | Metastasis or myeloma. Prostate is blastic. Most others are lytic. Myeloma is lytic and the bone scan can be cold. |

Skin: a melanocyte is the pigment cell. A basal-cell carcinoma is pearly and local. A squamous-cell carcinoma can come from an actinic keratosis. Melanoma depth is what the path report is for. Pemphigus is flaccid bullae and intraepidermal split (desmoglein). Bullous pemphigoid is tense bullae and a subepidermal split (hemidesmosome). Nikolsky is the pemphigus story.

## Checklist

- [ ] Antibody only after the clinical pattern.
- [ ] Osteosarcoma is the teen metaphysis.
- [ ] Myeloma can have a cold bone scan.
- [ ] Pemphigus is inside the epidermis. Pemphigoid is under it.
- [ ] Proximal weakness is muscle. Pain without weakness is often joint or bone.

## Common traps

- Ordering anti-dsDNA as a screening test for everyone. The pattern comes first.
- Calling every childhood bone pain Ewing. Site and the x-ray words matter.
- Treating bullous pemphigoid as pemphigus because both blister.
