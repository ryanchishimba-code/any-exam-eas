# Exam day

## The day

The bulletin on usmle.org is the source of truth for arrival, breaks, and what you may bring. This chapter does not invent a minute count or a block count. Read the current bulletin the week you test, not a forum summary.

**The night before**

- Sleep on your ordinary schedule. A new sleep trick is an experiment you do not run tonight.
- Do not start a new drug, a new diet, or a new chapter of trivia.
- Lay out the identification the bulletin requires.

**During the form**

1. Read the last line of the stem first when the story is long. Know what is being asked.
2. Cross out answers that reverse the mechanism.
3. Mark and move when two answers remain and a minute is gone. A blank item cannot earn a point. Confirm the current interface rules in the bulletin. Do not assume a tutorial you remember from a practice site.
4. Use breaks to eat and to stand. Do not use them to look up the last item.

**If you panic**

Name the system out loud in your head, then the process. If you still do not know, pick the mechanism that must be true and move. Spiraling on one item taxes the items you do know.

## Common traps

- Cramming a new pathway in the lobby.
- Reviewing a missed item on your phone on a break. If the bulletin forbids it, it is not a strategy.
- Changing a run of answers to match a pattern. Forms are not patterned that way, and this book will not claim they are.

## Practice this in the Qbank

The week of the exam, do one timed [Step 1 block](/question-bank?field=usmle-step-1&mode=bank&count=25&pace=timed&subjectId=mixed) under the same rules you will use: last line first, mark and move. Then stop adding new topics.
