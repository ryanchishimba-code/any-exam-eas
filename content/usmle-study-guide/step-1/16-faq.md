# FAQ

## Answers

**Is this book affiliated with the USMLE, the NBME, or the FSMB?**

No. USMLE is their program. This is an independent study aid.

**Will this book make me pass?**

No. Nothing here promises a result. A finished checklist is not a score.

**Why is there no item count or pass rate?**

Those numbers change, and inventing them would be worse than omitting them. The bulletin and the score-reporting page are the source of truth.

**How is this different from the USMLE feed?**

The feed is one fact per card. This book is the framework that tells you which fact a vignette is using. Read the chapter, then drill the cards, then do a block.

**How is this different from the question bank?**

The bank is where you find out the framework fails under a clock. The chapter is not a substitute for that.

**Step 1 is pass/fail. Do mechanisms still matter?**

Yes. The report format does not change what the items test. Confirm the current reporting rule on the USMLE site.

**What if a range in chapter 1 disagrees with the website?**

Follow the website. The ranges were recorded on 2026-09-06.

**Can I use this for patient care?**

No. It is an exam map.

## Common traps

- Quoting this book to a patient.
- Skipping the bank because the checklists felt familiar.
- Studying only the feed cards and never building the map.
