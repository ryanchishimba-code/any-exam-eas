# Gastrointestinal mechanisms

## Framework

Follow a molecule from the mouth to the stool, and follow bilirubin from the hepatocyte to the duct.

**Where things are absorbed or digested**

| Place | What fails when it is gone |
| --- | --- |
| Stomach acid and intrinsic factor | B12 later, and iron likes acid. Pernicious anemia is intrinsic factor, not a diet story. |
| Duodenum | Iron, folate. Celiac hits the proximal small bowel first. |
| Jejunum | Most calories. |
| Terminal ileum | B12-intrinsic factor complex and bile acids. Crohn loves this site. Resection causes B12 deficiency and bile-acid diarrhea. |
| Colon | Water. A secretory diarrhea does not stop when the patient fasts. An osmotic one does. |

**Bilirubin**

| Pattern | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Unconjugated high, no urine bilirubin | Too much production (hemolysis) or the liver cannot conjugate (Gilbert, Crigler-Najjar). |
| Conjugated high, dark urine, pale stool if the duct is blocked | The liver conjugated it and cannot get it out, or the duct is blocked. |
| ALT and AST very high | Hepatocytes are dying now. |
| Alkaline phosphatase and GGT high | The duct is the problem. Bone alkaline phosphatase does not raise GGT. |

Hepatitis serology is a timeline, not a trivia card. Surface antigen means the virus is present. Surface antibody, without core IgM, means vaccine or resolved infection. Core IgM means acute infection. Core IgG with surface antigen means chronic infection. e antigen is a marker of replication, not a vaccine target.

Crohn can be anywhere, is transmural, and skips. Ulcerative colitis starts in the rectum and stays in the mucosa of the colon. Toxic megacolon and primary sclerosing cholangitis sit with ulcerative colitis on exams.

## Checklist

- [ ] Name the segment before the deficiency.
- [ ] Conjugated versus unconjugated before the disease.
- [ ] Surface antigen present means infection, not immunity.
- [ ] Osmotic diarrhea stops with fasting. Secretory does not.
- [ ] Terminal ileum is B12 and bile acids.

## Common traps

- Calling a high unconjugated bilirubin "obstruction." Obstruction is conjugated, and the stool goes pale.
- Treating celiac as a colon disease. It is proximal small bowel.
- Reading surface antibody as acute infection. Acute infection is core IgM.
