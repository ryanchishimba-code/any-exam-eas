# Nervous system and behavior

## Framework

Localize first. A drug or a transmitter is the second sentence.

**Where the lesion is**

| Finding | The localization |
| --- | --- |
| Face and body weak on the same side, aphasia or neglect | Cortex or the internal capsule on the opposite side. |
| Face spared or forehead spared, and a brainstem sign (crossed findings, a cranial nerve) | Brainstem. |
| One limb, a dermatome, no brain signs | Nerve, root, or cord. A cord gives a level. |
| Resting tremor, rigidity | Substantia nigra. Dopamine from the nigrostriatal path is down. |
| Intention tremor, falls to one side | Cerebellum or its connections. |

An upper motor neuron lesion gives weakness, spasticity, and a Babinski sign later. A lower motor neuron lesion gives weakness, low tone, fasciculations, and a lost reflex in that segment. Acute upper motor neuron lesions can be flaccid at first. Do not call that a nerve.

**Vessel, only as a territory**

| Vessel | What is lost |
| --- | --- |
| Middle cerebral | Face and arm more than leg. Language if it is the dominant side. |
| Anterior cerebral | Leg more than arm. |
| Posterior cerebral | Vision, often a field cut. The macula may be spared when the middle cerebral artery still feeds it. |
| Posterior inferior cerebellar | Wallenberg. Swallowing, a crossed pain-temperature loss. No weakness of the body is the point. |

**Transmitter diseases**

Parkinson disease is low dopamine. The treatment idea is replace dopamine or block acetylcholine's relative excess. Huntington is loss of the caudate's inhibitory output, so movement is too much. Alzheimer disease testing focuses on acetylcholine in the cortex for the drugs that exist, and on amyloid and tau for the pathology. Depression drugs that the exam cares about raise serotonin, norepinephrine, or both, or they block breakdown. A hypertensive crisis with tyramine is an MAO inhibitor problem. Serotonin syndrome is too much serotonin activity: clonus, heat, reflexes. That is not the lead-pipe rigidity of neuroleptic malignant syndrome.

## Checklist

- [ ] Localize before you name the disease.
- [ ] Crossed signs mean brainstem.
- [ ] Middle cerebral is face and arm. Anterior cerebral is leg.
- [ ] Parkinson is too little dopamine. Huntington is too much movement from lost inhibition.
- [ ] Serotonin syndrome has clonus. Neuroleptic malignant syndrome is rigid and after a dopamine blocker.

## Common traps

- Calling every facial weakness a stroke. Forehead sparing is an upper motor neuron of the face. The whole half of the face is a nerve.
- Giving a dopamine blocker to a patient with Parkinson disease for nausea. Metoclopramide does this.
- Treating Wallenberg as a hemiparesis. Motor power is often spared.
