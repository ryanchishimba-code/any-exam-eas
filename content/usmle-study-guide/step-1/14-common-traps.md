# Traps that cross systems

## Framework

These are the misses that do not live in one organ chapter. Each one is a mechanism you already have. The trap is answering a neighbor question.

| You see | The wrong reflex | The correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low pH and high CO2 | "Give bicarbonate" | The lungs are the cause. Fix ventilation. |
| Child, small red cells, normal iron studies | Iron | Thalassemia trait until the studies say otherwise. |
| Warm shock, low pressure | More fluid forever | After volume, the pipes are open. The Step 2 book covers the vasopressor. Here, name distributive shock. |
| Antibody positive, story negative | Treat the titer | The story picks the disease. The antibody confirms. |
| Drug level low on an inducer | Resistance | The enzyme ate the drug. |
| Family wants you to hide a diagnosis from a capable adult | Kindness | The patient decides what to hear. |
| Screening survival looks better | The test saves lives | Ask about lead time and about deaths, not only about years since diagnosis. |
| Mitochondrial pedigree with an affected father transmitting | "Variable" | Fathers do not transmit mitochondrial disease. Re-read the pedigree. |

## Checklist

- [ ] Say the mechanism in one sentence before you look at the choices.
- [ ] If two answers are true, read the last line of the stem again. It asks for cause, consequence, or the next molecular step.
- [ ] A famous toxicity is allowed only when the organ matches the drug's mechanism.
- [ ] Do not import a Step 2 treatment algorithm into a mechanism item.
- [ ] Do not import a mechanism into a "what is the diagnosis" item and stop there if the stem asked for the process.

## Common traps

- Changing an answer because a later item used the same disease.
- Adding a drug side effect you memorized from a different drug in the same class.
- Treating the longest choice as the most complete one.
