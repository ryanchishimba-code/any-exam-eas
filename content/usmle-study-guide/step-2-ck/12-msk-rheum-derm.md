# Musculoskeletal, rheumatology, and skin

## Framework

Septic until proven otherwise when a joint is hot and the patient is sick. Crystals and autoimmune disease are what remain after the tap.

| Joint | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| Hot joint, fever | Tap before antibiotics if you can do it immediately. Do not start a week of oral antibiotics and "see." |
| Podagra, first metatarsophalangeal joint, not septic | Treat the gout flare. Do not start allopurinol in the middle of the first flare and walk away. Urate-lowering therapy is a separate decision when flares recur or tophi are present (ACR gout guidance). |
| Back pain with bowel or bladder change, saddle anesthesia | Emergency imaging. This is cord or cauda equina, not physical therapy. |
| Back pain, no red flags | Move. Imaging is not the first week. |
| Temporal artery story | Steroids. See the neurology chapter. |

Rheumatoid arthritis is a DMARD disease, often methotrexate if the organs allow, not a lifetime of steroids. Lupus nephritis is not "more hydroxychloroquine and hope." Septic arthritis can sit on top of a known crystal disease. The tap still happens.

Skin emergencies: a denuding drug rash with mucosal involvement is Stevens-Johnson or toxic epidermal necrolysis. Stop the drug. This is not a course of the same antibiotic "because the rash is from the infection." Necrotizing fasciitis is an operation. Melanoma is excision. A pearly papule is basal cell and is still excised, but it is not the same conversation as a changing black lesion.

Osteoporosis screening: women 65 and older, and younger postmenopausal women at increased risk (USPSTF). A T-score of −2.5 or lower is osteoporosis. Do not stop denosumab without a follow-on plan. Rebound vertebral fractures are the point.

## Checklist

- [ ] Tap a hot joint.
- [ ] Cauda equina is not a clinic follow-up.
- [ ] Uncomplicated back pain does not start with an MRI.
- [ ] Stop the culprit drug in a mucosal rash.
- [ ] Do not abruptly stop denosumab.

## Common traps

- Allopurinol as the treatment of an acute flare and nothing else.
- Steroids forever for rheumatoid arthritis.
- Antibiotics for a hot joint without a culture plan.
