# Exam day

## The day

The Step 2 CK bulletin is the source of truth for identification, breaks, and what the testing center allows. This chapter does not invent a block count or a minute count.

1. Read the last line. Diagnosis, test, or treatment.
2. If the patient is unstable, the answer is the stabilizing act.
3. If the patient is stable, do not pick an ICU drug to look thorough.
4. Mark and move. Confirm how marking works in the current tutorial. Do not trust a memory of a practice website.
5. Eat on breaks. Do not look up the last item.

The night before is for sleep, not for a new guideline PDF.

## Common traps

- Changing answers to balance the letter pattern.
- Adding a new drug class in the parking lot.
- Spending the break re-arguing one item you cannot change.
