# Exam day for Step 2 CK

> **Study aid only.** The Bulletin wins when this page is stale. Nothing here promises a result.

## What this chapter covers

The published shape of the day and a way to spend it. Step 2 CK is one day, about 9 hours, about 316 items in sixteen 30-minute blocks, no more than 20 items in a block, at least 55 minutes of break, and a 5-minute optional tutorial. Early finishes become break. Confirm on usmle.org.

## The framework

The blocks are shorter than a ward round and longer than your attention will like by block twelve. The method does not change when you are tired.

| Moment | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Tutorial | Skip it if you know the official interface. Take the minutes as break. |
| Stem | Read the last line first so you know whether they want a diagnosis, a test, or a treatment. |
| Unstable patient | If the stem says unstable, the answer is the resuscitation, not the elegant test. |
| Two right answers | Pick the one that changes today’s management. A true statement that does not help this patient is wrong. |
| Break | Food, walk, no postmortem of a submitted block. |
| Ethics item late in the day | Slow down. Fatigue makes people pick the socially easy wrong answer. |

You may sit Step 1 and Step 2 CK in either order if you are eligible. Do not invent a strategy from that permission. Eligibility and scheduling are the Bulletin and the registration entity (NBME, or ECFMG for many international graduates).

If the day breaks, tell the test center that day and write the USMLE program inside the Bulletin’s window. A survey comment is not notice.

## Checklist

- [ ] Official interface practiced, so the tutorial is optional.
- [ ] Break food that you have eaten before on a long day.
- [ ] Last line of the stem first.
- [ ] No new guideline PDF the morning of the exam.
- [ ] Bulletin re-read for what you may bring and what ID works.

## Common traps

- Changing an answer from a guideline-based choice to a “more thorough” workup. Thorough is often the trap.
- Skipping breaks and fading in the last four blocks.
- Treating a practice percent as a prediction. Forms differ.

## Practice this in the Qbank

One full timed block of 30 minutes, then a five-minute break, then another. The skill is restarting. The feed is for a card you already own, not for a new exception on the sidewalk outside the test center.
