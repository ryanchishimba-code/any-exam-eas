# Women's health and obstetrics

> **Study aid only.** ACOG is the source for pregnancy care. USPSTF is the source for the screening ages below. They disagree sometimes with specialty societies. The exam usually wants the preventive-services answer unless the stem is clearly a specialist problem. Re-check ages. They move.

## What this chapter covers

Prenatal danger signs, the hypertensive disorders of pregnancy, bleeding in pregnancy, and the screening and contraception decisions of outpatient gynecology. Reproductive systems are grouped with renal at about 7–13%. Pregnancy itself is about 3–7%. Obstetrics and gynecology as a clinical science is about 10–20%. This chapter is sized for that, not for the small-looking pregnancy row alone.

## The framework

Pregnancy care is a calendar plus a list of things that are never normal. Bleeding with pain in early pregnancy is ectopic until an intrauterine pregnancy is seen. Painless third-trimester bleeding is placenta previa until the ultrasound says the placenta is clear of the cervix, and you do not do a digital exam. Painful third-trimester bleeding is abruption until proven otherwise, and the mother can be in shock before the vaginal blood looks impressive.

| Problem | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Hypertensive disorder of pregnancy | ACOG: gestational hypertension, preeclampsia without severe features, and preeclampsia with severe features are different clocks. Severe features (very high pressure, symptoms, lab injury, pulmonary edema) mean delivery planning and magnesium for seizure prevention, not a casual outpatient follow-up. Magnesium toxicity is lost reflexes, then respiratory depression. Calcium gluconate is the antidote. |
| Eclampsia | The seizure. Magnesium, airway, and delivery. |
| GBS | Screen late in pregnancy as ACOG directs, and treat in labor when the screen is positive or when a risk factor says to. This prevents early neonatal sepsis. |
| Rh-negative mother | Anti-D immunoglobulin at the routine antenatal time and after any bleeding or procedure that can mix blood. A positive antibody screen is a different, higher-risk path. |
| Gestational diabetes | Screen as ACOG directs, usually mid-pregnancy, earlier if risk is high. The thresholds are the Carpenter-Coustan or the one-step criteria your protocol chose. Do not mix the numbers from the two methods. |
| Postpartum hemorrhage | Tone, trauma, tissue, thrombin. Uterine massage and uterotonics for atony. Look at the laceration and the placenta. |
| Contraception | The CDC Medical Eligibility Criteria say who cannot use estrogen (migraine with aura, smoking over age 35, clot history, some postpartum days). A long-acting reversible method is the most effective. Emergency contraception is time-sensitive and is not an abortifacient in the way a medical abortion is. Do not conflate them. |
| Breast screening | USPSTF: biennial mammography for women 40 to 74 (2024, grade B). Evidence is insufficient at 75 and older. |
| Cervical screening | USPSTF (2018, still the cited recommendation until they replace it): cytology every 3 years from 21 to 29. From 30 to 65, cytology every 3 years, primary HPV every 5 years, or co-testing every 5 years. |
| Intimate-partner violence | Ask. Offer a plan the person controls. Do not hand them a pamphlet in front of the person who came with them. |

Prenatal labs that change management: blood type and antibody screen, hemoglobin, HIV, syphilis, hepatitis B, rubella immunity, and the later GBS and diabetes screens. A positive syphilis or HIV test in pregnancy is treatment, not a recheck next month. Congenital syphilis and perinatal HIV are preventable and still happen when someone delays.

Ectopic precautions are a script: come back for dizziness, shoulder pain, worsening pain, or heavy bleeding. A methotrexate candidate must be able to follow up. A ruptured ectopic is surgery.

## Checklist

- [ ] No digital exam when previa is possible.
- [ ] Severe features of preeclampsia change the clock. Magnesium prevents the seizure.
- [ ] Do not mix one-step and two-step diabetes numbers.
- [ ] Estrogen is not for everyone. Open the CDC eligibility chart.
- [ ] Mammography 40 to 74, every 2 years, is the current USPSTF grade B.

## Common traps

- Treating blood pressure of 148/96 in pregnancy with the adult 2025 PREVENT rules. Different disease.
- Methylergonovine in a hypertensive patient with atony. It raises pressure. Use a different uterotonic.
- A Pap smear before age 21 because the patient is sexually active. USPSTF starts at 21.
- Emergency contraception confused with a medication abortion. Different drugs, different purpose, different follow-up.

## Practice this in the Qbank

Obstetric items are a trimester plus a danger sign. Gynecologic items are a screening age or a contraindication. Feed cards on “start mammograms at 50” are out of date relative to the 2024 USPSTF recommendation. Retire them.
