# Infectious disease

## Framework

Source, host, and the drug that covers the organism you are actually worried about. "Broad spectrum" is not an answer.

| Situation | The move |
| --- | --- |
| Sepsis with a source you can name | Cultures if they will not delay the drug, then the antibiotic, then source control. Fluids if the pressure is low. Norepinephrine if fluids fail. Confirm the current Surviving Sepsis timing. Do not wait on a pretty culture while the pressure falls. |
| Uncomplicated cystitis | Nitrofurantoin, trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole, or fosfomycin when resistance allows (IDSA). Do not treat asymptomatic bacteriuria except in pregnancy and before a urologic procedure where bleeding is expected. |
| Cervicitis or urethritis, chlamydia | Doxycycline 100 mg by mouth twice daily for 7 days. Not azithromycin as the preferred regimen. ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/std/treatment-guidelines/chlamydia.htm)) |
| Gonorrhea of the cervix, urethra, or rectum | Ceftriaxone 500 mg intramuscularly once if under 150 kg, and 1 g if 150 kg or more. ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/std/treatment-guidelines/gonorrhea-adults.htm)) |
| Early syphilis | Benzathine penicillin G 2.4 million units intramuscularly once. Penicillin is the pregnancy drug. Confirm the current CDC page if the shortage guidance has changed. |
| HIV, newly diagnosed | Start antiretroviral therapy. Do not wait for a low CD4. Screen adults at least once (USPSTF). Below a CD4 of 200, Pneumocystis prophylaxis, usually trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole. |
| Neutropenic fever | Antipseudomonal beta-lactam promptly. Add vancomycin for a line infection, severe mucositis, known MRSA, or shock (IDSA). |
| C. difficile | Oral vancomycin or fidaxomicin. Not metronidazole as first line. |

Cellulitis without pus is streptococcal until proven otherwise. Purulent disease makes you think about MRSA. Necrotizing infection (pain out of proportion, bullae, gas, systemic toxicity) is an operating room, not another day of oral antibiotics.

## Checklist

- [ ] Asymptomatic bacteriuria is not a UTI, except pregnancy and selected procedures.
- [ ] Doxycycline for chlamydia in adolescents and adults.
- [ ] Ceftriaxone dose for gonorrhea depends on weight.
- [ ] Start HIV treatment when you diagnose it.
- [ ] Neutropenic fever gets a pseudomonal drug now.

## Common traps

- Azithromycin as the preferred chlamydia regimen.
- Metronidazole first for C. difficile.
- A urine culture in an asymptomatic older adult, then treating the result.
