# Psychiatry

## Framework

Safety first. Then the syndrome. Then the drug that matches the syndrome, not the drug you remember.

USPSTF recommends depression screening in adults, including pregnant and postpartum patients. A positive screen is an interview, not a prescription. A patient with a plan, intent, or a recent attempt is not a screening question. That patient needs a safety evaluation now. Asking about suicide does not plant the idea. ([USPSTF depression screening](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/screening-depression-in-adults))

| Syndrome | The drug fact the exam wants |
| --- | --- |
| Depression, first line | An SSRI or another first-line antidepressant, plus therapy. Not a benzodiazepine as treatment of the illness. |
| Mania | A mood stabilizer or an antipsychotic. An antidepressant alone can worsen mania. |
| Psychosis | An antipsychotic. Clozapine is for treatment-resistant illness, and it requires neutrophil monitoring. It is not a first prescription. |
| Alcohol withdrawal, autonomic storm | A benzodiazepine. Thiamine before glucose. |
| Opioid overdose, slow breathing | Naloxone. |
| Serotonin syndrome | Stop the drugs. Support. A serotonin antagonist is for severe cases. Clonus is the clue. |
| Neuroleptic malignant syndrome | Stop the dopamine blocker. Rigidity and a very high CK. This is not serotonin syndrome. |

Anorexia that is unstable (bradycardia, electrolyte chaos, syncope) is an admission. Refeeding can drop phosphate. Watch it. Bulimia with a normal weight still gets an SSRI discussion (fluoxetine is the one with the evidence most exams mean) plus therapy. Do not pick bupropion in an eating disorder that includes purging or a seizure risk.

## Checklist

- [ ] Safety before the antidepressant.
- [ ] Thiamine before glucose in alcohol use disorder.
- [ ] Naloxone for opioid apnea.
- [ ] Clozapine is not step one.
- [ ] Screen is not a diagnosis.

## Common traps

- An antidepressant alone for bipolar depression in a stem that is actually mania.
- Benzodiazepine as the treatment of depression.
- Ignoring phosphate during refeeding.
