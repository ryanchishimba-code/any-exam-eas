# How to use the Step 2 CK book

**Study aid only.** This is not medical advice and not a promise of a result. AnyExamEasy is independent of the USMLE program, the NBME, and the FSMB.

Step 2 CK asks for the diagnosis and the next step. The feed cards hold one decision each. This book is the order you make those decisions in. The question bank is where the order fails under a clock.

## Study plan

Medicine is the largest clinical share on the recorded outline (about 55–65%, and the ranges overlap with other fields). Pediatrics, obstetrics and gynecology, and psychiatry are the next blocks you cannot skip. Surgery is smaller and still shows up as the next procedure, not as a technique.

| Plan | Shape |
| --- | --- |
| 2 weeks | One heavy system a day, then a timed block. Stop adding facts two days out. |
| 6 weeks | Two systems a week. Every miss becomes a next-step sentence. |
| 12 weeks | One system a week, with a mixed block twice a week so you do not forget the first system. |

**The only framework you reuse**

1. How sick is this person, right now?
2. What is the single most likely diagnosis?
3. What test changes the next order?
4. What is the next order, not the eventual therapy?

## Common traps

- Ordering the most thorough test instead of the next test.
- Treating a stable patient with an ICU drug because the disease name sounds serious.
- Reading this book as a protocol for your patients. It is an exam map. Confirm the live guideline before you act.
