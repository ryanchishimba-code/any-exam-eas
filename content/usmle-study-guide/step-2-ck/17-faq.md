# Step 2 CK questions people ask

> **Study aid only.** The official page wins a disagreement. This book does not promise a pass.

## What this chapter covers

What this Step is for, how the books and the feed fit together, and the mistakes that come from using a Step 1 habit on a Step 2 item.

## The framework

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| What is Step 2 CK for? | Care under supervision: diagnosis, studies, treatment, and prevention. The USMLE program says so. |
| Who writes it? | USMLE committees. The program belongs to the FSMB and the NBME. AnyExamEasy does not. |
| Why does nutrition look so large? | It is a discipline double-coded to systems, not an extra organ worth a fifth of the day by itself. USMLE explains this on the specifications page. |
| Do I quote a guideline by year? | Yes, when you can. Then check that the year is still current before you use it on a person. |
| What if USPSTF and a specialty society disagree? | Know which one you are using. Screening items often follow USPSTF. A specialist stem may follow the specialty. The stem tells you the setting. |
| Where are the mechanisms? | In the Step 1 book. Use them when an item asks why. Do not stop at why when it asks what you do. |
| Does the feed replace this book? | No. A card is one decision. A chapter is the set of decisions and the reason you throw some of them out. |
| Will this get me a score? | No one can say that. The passing standard is published by USMLE and can change. Look it up. Do not take a number from a forum or from this FAQ. |
| What about Step 2 CS? | It is not part of the current USMLE sequence. Do not study for an exam that is not offered. Confirm on usmle.org if you have heard otherwise. |

## Checklist

- [ ] You can separate a diagnosis item from a next-step item by the last line.
- [ ] You know nutrition is double-coded.
- [ ] You are not studying for a clinical-skills exam that is not on the schedule.
- [ ] Your guideline years are written down so you can retire them.
- [ ] You have not stored a passing score in your notes.

## Common traps

- A beautiful mechanism as the answer to “most appropriate next step.”
- An old screening age defended with “that’s what I learned.”
- Using Step 3 CCS habits (order everything) on a one-best-answer item. The one best answer is narrower.

## Practice this in the Qbank

Readiness is mixed timed blocks you have not seen. Sort the misses. Let the misses pick the chapter. Let the chapter pick the card. The other direction is how people drown in content.
