# FAQ

## Answers

**Is this affiliated with the NBME or the FSMB?**

No.

**Does finishing the checklists mean I will pass?**

No. This book does not promise a result and does not quote a pass rate.

**Where are the passing score and the item count?**

On the USMLE site. They are omitted here so this book cannot invent them.

**How does this sit next to the feed and the bank?**

Feed: one decision. Book: the order of decisions. Bank: the order under a clock.

**What if a guideline in a chapter is updated?**

Follow the linked page. The year named in the chapter is the edition this text was checked against. CLINICAL-REVIEW.md lists the thresholds a physician should recheck.

**Can I use a chapter as an order set?**

No.

## Common traps

- Quoting a chapter to a patient.
- Skipping pediatrics and ethics because medicine feels bigger.
- Treating a relative risk in a practice abstract as a personal probability.
