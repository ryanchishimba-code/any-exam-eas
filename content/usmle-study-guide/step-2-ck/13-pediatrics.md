# Pediatrics

## Framework

Step 2 pediatrics is development, the sick infant, and the screen you do not skip. Doses are not your job in this book. The decision is.

| Age clue | What the item is testing |
| --- | --- |
| Newborn, bilious vomiting | Malrotation until a upper GI study says otherwise. This is not a week of reflux precautions. |
| Infant, olive mass, projectile nonbilious vomiting | Pyloric stenosis. Correct electrolytes, then surgery. The surgery is not before the potassium. |
| Child, drooling, fever, leaning forward | Epiglottitis. Airway in a controlled setting. Do not look with a tongue blade in the hallway. |
| Barking cough, well-appearing | Croup. Dexamethasone. Racemic epinephrine if stridor at rest. |
| Fever, petechiae, ill | Do not send home. Meningococcus is the fear. |
| Infant under 28 days with fever | Full evaluation. This is not "watch at home" the way an older vaccinated child sometimes is. Follow the current AAP febrile-infant pathway. The age cutoffs have been updated. Do not quote a cutoff from memory if you have not checked. |
| Limp after a viral illness, well, hip | Transient synovitis is common. Septic hip is the one you must not miss. Kocher features and a pediatric exam still end at a tap when the story is hot. |
| Developmental regression | Not a normal variant. Metabolic and neurologic evaluation. |

Vaccines follow the current CDC schedule. The exam mechanism is: a live vaccine is not for a child who cannot control a live organism. Anaphylaxis to a component is a real contraindication. A mild cold is not.

## Checklist

- [ ] Bilious vomiting in a newborn is a surgical emergency until proven otherwise.
- [ ] Fix pyloric electrolytes before the operating room.
- [ ] Do not instrument a suspected epiglottitis airway in the hallway.
- [ ] A neonate with fever is not an older child.
- [ ] Regression is not "he'll catch up."

## Common traps

- Reflux precautions for bilious emesis.
- A tongue blade in epiglottitis.
- Delaying pyloric surgery for a perfect weight while the potassium is 2.
