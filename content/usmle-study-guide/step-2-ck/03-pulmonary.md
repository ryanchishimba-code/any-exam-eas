# Pulmonary

## Framework

Separate the diseases that need a bronchodilator plan from the diseases that need a clot, a bacterium, or a tube.

**Asthma, GINA track for ages 12 and older**

Do not leave a patient on a short-acting beta-agonist alone. Steps 1 and 2 of Track 1 are as-needed low-dose inhaled corticosteroid-formoterol. Step 3 is low-dose maintenance and reliever with the same combination. Step 4 raises the maintenance dose. Step 5 adds a long-acting muscarinic antagonist and refers. A severe attack (cannot speak, silent chest, hypoxia) needs oxygen, repeated short-acting bronchodilator, and a systemic corticosteroid. ([GINA](https://ginasthma.org/))

**COPD, GOLD**

Diagnosis is symptoms plus a post-bronchodilator FEV1/FVC under 0.7. Group A starts with a bronchodilator. Group B (more symptoms: mMRC 2 or higher, or CAT 10 or higher) starts with a LABA plus a LAMA. Group E (two or more moderate exacerbations, or one hospitalization) starts with a LABA plus a LAMA, and an inhaled corticosteroid is considered when eosinophils are 300/µL or higher. Smoking cessation changes the course. In a patient at risk of retaining carbon dioxide, target oxygen saturation about 88 to 92%. ([GOLD](https://goldcopd.org/))

**The emergencies**

| Picture | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| Sudden dyspnea, clear lungs, risk for clot | Think pulmonary embolism. A stable patient with low risk can be ruled out with criteria plus a D-dimer. A high-risk or unstable patient is not a D-dimer case. Anticoagulate when the diagnosis is likely and bleeding risk allows. Confirm the current PE guideline pathway. |
| Community pneumonia, healthy outpatient | Amoxicillin, doxycycline, or a macrolide only where pneumococcal resistance is low (IDSA/ATS). Do not reach for a fluoroquinolone first in a healthy adult. |
| Tension pneumothorax | Needle decompression then a chest tube. The x-ray is not the next step when the pressure is gone. |
| TB risk, cough, upper-lobe cavity | Airborne isolation and sputum. Do not start one drug. |

Lung cancer screening, USPSTF: annual low-dose CT for adults 50 to 80 with a 20 pack-year history who smoke or quit within 15 years. Stop when the person has not smoked for 15 years or when they cannot tolerate curative treatment. ([USPSTF](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/lung-cancer-screening))

## Checklist

- [ ] No short-acting beta-agonist as the only asthma treatment.
- [ ] Group E COPD starts with LABA plus LAMA.
- [ ] Healthy outpatient pneumonia is not a fluoroquinolone reflex.
- [ ] Tension physiology is a needle, not a film.
- [ ] Low-dose CT screening is a pack-year rule, not "every smoker."

## Common traps

- Steroids alone for an asthma attack, without a bronchodilator.
- High-flow oxygen in a chronic retainer "to get the saturation to 100%."
- Repeating a negative chest x-ray as if it rules out a pulmonary embolism.
