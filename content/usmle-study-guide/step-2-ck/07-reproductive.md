# Reproductive and obstetric

## Framework

Pregnancy items are timing plus one dangerous branch. Gynecology items are bleeding, a mass, or a screen.

**Prenatal timing used on exams (ACOG)**

Blood type and antibody screen, hemoglobin, rubella, hepatitis B surface antigen, HIV, syphilis, and a urine culture in the first trimester. Anatomy ultrasound around 18 to 22 weeks. Gestational diabetes screening at 24 to 28 weeks. Anti-D immune globulin at about 28 weeks if the patient is Rh-negative and unsensitized, and again after an Rh-positive delivery or a bleed. Group B streptococcus culture at 36 to 37 weeks. Intrapartum penicillin if the culture is positive, if a prior infant had invasive group B disease, or if status is unknown and there is fever, prolonged rupture, or prematurity. Tdap in every pregnancy at 27 to 36 weeks (ACIP).

**Hypertension in pregnancy (ACOG Practice Bulletin 222)**

Preeclampsia is new hypertension at or after 20 weeks plus proteinuria, or new hypertension plus a severe feature (platelets under 100,000, creatinine up, liver enzymes twice normal, pulmonary edema, a new cerebral or visual symptom, or blood pressure at or above 160/110). Magnesium is for seizures, not for blood pressure. Severe pressure is treated with intravenous labetalol, intravenous hydralazine, or immediate-release oral nifedipine. Delivery is the cure. ACE inhibitors and ARBs are contraindicated in pregnancy.

**Bleeding and screening**

First-trimester bleeding with a closed cervix and a live pregnancy is threatened abortion. An open cervix is inevitable. Unstable bleeding goes to evacuation. Stable patients may choose expectant care, misoprostol, or uterine aspiration (ACOG).

Postpartum hemorrhage is usually atony: a boggy uterus. Massage and oxytocin. Add another uterotonic that the patient's lungs and blood pressure allow. A firm uterus that is bleeding is trauma until you look.

Mammogram every 2 years from 40 through 74 (USPSTF). A palpable mass is diagnostic imaging, not a screening mammogram. Cervical screening: cytology every 3 years from 21 through 29. From 30 through 65, cytology every 3 years, high-risk HPV every 5 years, or cotesting every 5 years. Do not start before 21. Stop after 65 when recent screens were adequate and negative. ([USPSTF breast](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/breast-cancer-screening), [USPSTF cervical](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/cervical-cancer-screening))

## Checklist

- [ ] Magnesium for preeclampsia seizures. A pressure drug for the pressure.
- [ ] No ACE inhibitor in pregnancy.
- [ ] Group B culture at 36 to 37 weeks, penicillin in labor when indicated.
- [ ] Screening mammogram is not the test for a lump.
- [ ] Cervical screening does not start before 21.

## Common traps

- Magnesium as an antihypertensive.
- Methylergonovine in a patient with hypertension.
- A screening mammogram for a palpable mass.
