# Chapter 5 — Infectious Disease

> **Study aid only.** Empiric starts, classic traps, and HIV thresholds. Confirm local resistance and current guidelines.

## Why this chapter matters

ID vignettes reward **syndrome + severity → first regimen**, then narrowing. Cultures before antibiotics when the patient is stable enough; never beyond the hour-1 window in sepsis or meningitis.

## Core concepts

- **CAP outpatient (healthy):** Amoxicillin, doxycycline, or a macrolide if local macrolide resistance is low.
- **CAP inpatient non-ICU:** Ceftriaxone + azithromycin, or a respiratory fluoroquinolone.
- **CAP ICU / MRSA risk:** Add vancomycin or linezolid. **Never daptomycin for pneumonia** — pulmonary surfactant inactivates it.
- **Bacterial meningitis:** Ceftriaxone + vancomycin ± ampicillin (age >50, neonate, pregnant, immunocompromised). Dexamethasone 0.15 mg/kg q6h ×4 with or just before the first antibiotic dose when pneumococcus is likely.
- **Do not delay antibiotics** for CT/LP if meningitis is likely and imaging will stall care.
- **C. diff first episode:** Oral vancomycin or fidaxomicin. Stop the inciting antibiotic. Contact precautions.
- **HIV OI prophylaxis:** TMP-SMX at CD4 <200 (PCP; toxoplasma if IgG+). MAC prophylaxis is no longer routine in many settings when ART is started promptly — verify the current bulletin you are using for the exam year.
- **Febrile neutropenia (ANC <500, T ≥38.3°C):** Two blood cultures, then an anti-pseudomonal beta-lactam within 60 minutes.
- **Vancomycin:** AUC/MIC 400–600 is the modern target; trough 15–20 is a surrogate.

## Clinical applications

- 65-year-old CAP, not ICU — start empiric inpatient coverage; do not wait on sputum.
- Fever, nuchal rigidity, LP delayed for CT — steroids + antibiotics first.
- Line-associated MRSA bacteremia — source control + vancomycin or daptomycin; echo to look for endocarditis.
- Watery diarrhea after clindamycin — oral vancomycin, not IV metronidazole as first-line.

## Trap table

| Trap | Why it fails | Do this instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daptomycin for MRSA pneumonia | Inactivated by surfactant | Vancomycin or linezolid |
| IV metronidazole first for C. diff | Poor colonic levels | Oral vanco or fidaxomicin |
| Waiting on LP in obvious meningitis | Time to antibiotic is the outcome | Treat, then image/LP |
| Daptomycin forgotten in bacteremia | Pneumonia is the exception, not all MRSA | Daptomycin is fine for blood/endocarditis/skin |

## Pearls

- Empiric first, then narrow.
- Blood-brain barrier drugs only for meningitis.
- Neutropenic fever is a timer, not a culture contest.
