She does not smoke and does not drink alcohol. Her weight is 71 kg. The parental hip fracture is her one clear risk factor. No clinical risk tool has been run, and she requests DXA this afternoon. Temperature is 36.6°C, pulse is 70/min, respirations are 14/min, and blood pressure is 128/78 mm Hg. The parent's hip fracture occurred 2 years ago, and the patient herself has no fracture.
Which of the following is the most appropriate screening plan?
A. Order hip or spine DXA today solely because a parent broke a hip
B. Use a clinical risk tool next; parental hip fracture alone does not order DXA
C. Ignore the parent's fracture because she is not yet 65
D. Count the parent's fracture only if she has already broken a hip herself
E. Scan the forearm and skip both the tool and the hip

## S2N-198

No risk tool has been scored. Temperature is 36.8°C, pulse is 82/min, respirations are 15/min, and blood pressure is 134/82 mm Hg. She describes excess drinking for 8 years, and no DXA is on file.
Which of the following is the most appropriate screening plan?
A. Order spine DXA now for every postmenopausal woman who drinks to excess
B. Excess alcohol counts only after age 65, so do nothing now
C. Excess alcohol is a risk factor; use a clinical risk tool before DXA
D. A T-score of -2.0 on a future scan is the only number that matters today
E. Repeat a clinical exam yearly and never use a risk tool or DXA

## S2N-199

Which of the following is the most appropriate screening plan?

B. Low body weight is not a listed risk factor, so offer nothing
C. Wait until age 65 even though a risk factor is already confirmed
D. Low body weight is a risk factor; use a clinical risk tool before any DXA
E. Measure only a wrist film because her weight is 46 kg

## S2N-200

Which of the following is the most appropriate screening plan?
A. Do no imaging; the tool replaces DXA in women younger than 65
B. Order DXA of the forearm because the tool was abnormal
C. Ignore the abnormal tool result until she turns 65
D. Repeat the tool yearly and never image a woman under 65
E. Order DXA of the hip or spine because the tool shows increased risk
